## Overview
The green island: Dublin's pubs, the sheer cliffs of Moher, the Ring of Kerry's looping road, the wild west of Dingle, and the quiet, ancient islands of the Aran.

## Where to go
Top places: Dublin, Cliffs of Moher, Ring of Kerry, Dingle, Wild Atlantic Way, Aran Islands, Cork.

## Landmarks & sights
- Cliffs of Moher
- Newgrange (UNESCO)
- the Rock of Cashel
- the Ring of Kerry

## Food & drink
- Irish stew
- seafood (Galway, the west)
- bacon & cabbage
- Guinness & cider

## Things to do
- hiking the Wild Atlantic Way
- the Aran Islands by boat
- trad pub sessions
- Dingle's beaches

## Where to stay

Stay options: Dublin City Center Hotel, Killarney Lakeside Hotel, Dingle Bay Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best weather: May–September
- Rent a car — the west is best by road
