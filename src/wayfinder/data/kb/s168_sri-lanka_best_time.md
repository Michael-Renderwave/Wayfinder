## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** February, March, January — best overall: **February**.
- **Shoulder:** December, April, May.
- **Off-season:** June, July, August, September, October, November.
- Climate reference point: **Colombo** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- However, there are two major rainy seasons, each affecting different parts of the island.
- The main southwest “Yala” monsoon drenches the west, southwest, and the hill country from April to July.
- The less severe northeast “Maha” monsoon hits the east coast from November to March.
- As a result of the two non-overlapping monsoons, theres always good weather somewhere in Sri Lanka.
