## Overview
A volcanic island of waterfalls, black-sand beaches, glacier lagoons, and the northern lights — with Reykjavik's colorful harbor as the base for the ring road.

## Where to go
Top places: Reykjavík, Golden Circle, south coast, Westfjords, highlands, Snæfellsnes.

## Landmarks & sights
- Gullfoss waterfall
- Jökulsárlón glacier lagoon
- Snæfellsjökull volcano
- Reynisfjara black-sand beach

## Food & drink
- lamb
- skyur (cultured milk)
- seafood (langoustine)
- rye bread (rugbraud)

## Things to do
- whale watching (Hafnarfjordur)
- northern lights (Sept–March)
- glacier hikes
- hot springs & black-sand beaches

## Where to stay

Stay options: Reykjavik City Center Hotel, Vik South Coast Hotel, Isafjordur Westfjords Hotel.

## Good to know
- Northern lights: September–March, clear nights
- Weather changes fast — dress in layers
