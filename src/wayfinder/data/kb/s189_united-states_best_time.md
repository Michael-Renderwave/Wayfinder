## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** October, November, August — best overall: **October**.
- **Shoulder:** April, September, June.
- **Off-season:** May, July, March, February, January, December.
- Climate reference point: **Washington D.C.** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- The full amount of variation one can experience within the country ranges from Arctic tundra in Alaska to the tropical weather in Hawaii and South Florida.
- In the summer, it is hot and humid with temperatures just over 90 , heat indices around 100 , and dewpoints above 70 ; but from October through April the weather can range from 65 to short cold spells of 20 or so.
- The deserts of the Southwest (United States) are very hot and dry during the summer, the average summer maximum is just over 105 with record highs of 120 or so.
- Northeastern and Upper Southern cities are known for summers with temperatures reaching to 95 or higher, with record highs as high as 110 , with extremely high humidity, usually over 80% with dewpoints of 75 .
