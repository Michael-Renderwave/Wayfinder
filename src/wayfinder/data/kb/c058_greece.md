## Overview
The classic Mediterranean: Athens' Acropolis, the white-and-blue caldera of Santorini, Crete's long beaches, the cliff-hung monasteries of Meteora, and the wine islands of the Aegean.

## Where to go
Top places: Athens, Santorini, Crete, Mykonos, Meteora, Corfu, Peloponnese.

## Landmarks & sights
- the Acropolis (Athens) — 4,500-year-old citadel; Parthenon at the center; go at dawn or after 17:00 to beat crowds
- the Plaka district — whitewashed lanes below the Acropolis, artisan shops, tavernas
- Meteora monasteries — six monasteries perched atop sandstone pillars; Vazovis & Great Meteoron are the most accessible
- Santorini's caldera — Oia at sunset (famous), Fira for shopping, Ammoudi Bay for seafood
- Knossos palace (Crete) — 3,700-year-old Minoan palace; guided tours only
- Temple of Poseidon (Cap Sounion) — 6th-century BC columns on a cliff, 70 km south of Athens
- Delphi oracle site — mountain sanctuary, 3 hr from Athens
- the Old Town of Rhodes — 4 km of medieval walls, the Palace of the Grand Master
- Zakynthos (Zante) Navagio Beach — shipwreck on a pebble beach; access by boat only
- Kefalonia — Melissani Lake (underground, turquoise), Drogarati Gorge
- Mykonos — windmills, Little Venice, superclubs (Cavo Paradiso, Scorpios)
- Naxos — the longest beach in the Cyclades (Plaka), Mount Parnon hiking
- the Corfu Grotto — sea cave on the Ionian coast; boat tours from Kassiopi
- the Cave of the Waitos (Milos) — volcanic sea cave, 20 min boat from Adamas
- the Temple of Aphaia (Aegina) — best-preserved Doric temple, 40 min ferry from Athens

## Food & drink
- moussaka — layered eggplant & meat; try at Taverna Sarakino (Athens, Estia)
- souvlaki — pork or chicken on a stick; street food or taverna plates
- fresh seafood — grilled octopus, calamari; Ammoudi Bay (Santorini), Mikron Monas (Thessaloniki)
- Greek salad & baklava — everywhere; baklava at Theotokopoulos (Athens)
- spanakopita — spinach & feta phyllo pie; breakfast at any taverna
- gyro — the proper way is a pita wrap with tzatziki, tomato, onion
- fava — yellow split-pea purée; specialty of Tinos & Naxos
- loumousa — lamb in tomato & oregano; ask at any Cretan taverna
- retsina — resin-flavored wine; try at a winery in Santorini (Santo Wines, Venetsanos)
- frappe — thick iced coffee; the Greek invention
- loukoumades — honey donuts; street carts in Athens, Thessaloniki
- graviera cheese — aged sheep's cheese; try at a deli in Chania (Crete)

## Things to do
- Athens: 2-day combo of Acropolis + Plaka + National Museum + a sunset dinner on a rooftop
- sailing the Cyclades — 3–7 day catamaran; Athens (Piraeus) → Aegina → Mykonos → Naxos → Santorini
- beach hopping — Navagio (Zakynthos), Voutoumi (Milos), Elafonisi (Crete)
- Crete's Samaria gorge hike — 16 km one-way (4–5 hr), Europe's longest
- wine tasting — Santorini (Assyrtiko), Nemea (Agiorgitiko), Naoussa (Xinomavro)
- Thessaloniki food crawl — the White Tower, the waterfront, Ano Poli (upper town)
- hot springs — Nemia (Crete), the volcanic pools of Milos
- diving the Aegean — shipwrecks near Kassandria (Halkidiki)
- mountain hiking — Mount Olympus (Thessaly), Kili Farsalia (Greece's highest peak)
- the 24-hour cafe culture — try a taverna that never closes (Athens, Mykonos)

## Where to stay

Stay options:
- Athens: The Westin (Syntagma, luxury), Athens Plaza (Plaka, mid), Ibis Athens (budget)
- Santorini: Canaves (Oia, top), Grace Hotel (Fira, mid), Caveland Hostel (Imerovigli, budget)
- Crete: Grace Hotel (Hersonissos), Anemos Hotel (Chania Old Town, mid), Crete Suites (Rethymno, budget)
- Mykonos: The Onyx (Ornos, top), Astir Palace (Telendos, mid), Little Venice Hostel (Chora, budget)
- Thessaloniki: Divani (Palamis, mid), Grand Hotel (center, mid), Hotel Asty (budget)
- Rhodes: The Domes (Lindos, top), Amnisos (Kos, mid), Ibis Rhodes (budget)

## Good to know
- High season: June–August — book islands early
- Ferries are the lifeline — check schedules
