## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Madagascar has two seasons: a hot, rainy season from November to April; and a cooler, dry season from May to October.
- This region is notorious not only for a hot, humid climate in which tropical fevers are endemic but also for the destructive cyclones that occur during the rainy season, coming in principally from the direction of the Mascarene Islands.
- Thunderstorms are common during the rainy season in the central highlands, and lightning is a serious hazard.
- The dry season is pleasant and sunny, although somewhat chilly, especially in the mornings.
