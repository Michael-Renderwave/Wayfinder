## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** December, November, October — best overall: **December**.
- **Shoulder:** March, February, April.
- **Off-season:** January, May, September, June, July, August.
- Climate reference point: **Riyadh** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- The coasts are only slightly moderated by the sea, which usually keeps temperatures below 38 °C/100 °F, but at the price of extreme humidity (85-100%), which many find even more uncomfortable than the dry heat of the interior, especially at night.
- Daytime highs in Riyadh in December average only 21 °C/70 °F, and temperatures can easily fall below zero at night, occasionally even resulting in a sprinkling of snow in the southern mountains.
- The end of spring (April and May) is also a rainy season for much of the country.
- In the south, though, this pattern is reversed, with most rain falling during the Indian Oceans monsoon season between May and October.
