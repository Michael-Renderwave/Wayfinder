## Overview
The Carnival island: Trinidad's drumming, steel pans, and food (doubles, pelau), and Tobago's Seven Mile Beach — island life with a pulse.

## Where to go
Top places: Port of Spain, Tobago, Arima.

## Landmarks & sights
- Seven Mile Beach (Tobago)
- Arima Carnival grounds

## Food & drink
- pelau (okra stew)
- roti
- fresh crab & local rum

## Things to do
- Carnival (Feb/Mar)
- beach + birding (Tobago)
- cricket & reggae
- rum tours (Tobago)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Port of Spain City Hotel, Tobago Seven Mile Beach Hotel.

## Good to know
- Carnival (Feb/Mar) is the highlight — book months ahead
- Best: December–April (calm)
