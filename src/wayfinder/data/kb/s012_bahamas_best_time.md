## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** January, February, April — best overall: **January**.
- **Shoulder:** March, December, November.
- **Off-season:** May, July, June, August, October, September.
- Climate reference point: **Nassau** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- During summer months, temperature in the Bahamas rarely goes above 90°F (32°C).
- The normal climate during winter is mild with temperatures around 60°F (16°C).
- The Northern and Western islands, Grand Bahama Island, Great Abaco, Andros and Eleuthera are somewhat cooler than the southern islands.
- The Bahamas hurricane season runs from June to November, and rain storms are expected during this period.
