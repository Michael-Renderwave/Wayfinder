## Overview
The Inca heartland: Machu Picchu, Andean treks, Lima's world-class food, and the Amazon — one of the Americas' greatest travel countries.

## Where to go
Top places: Lima, Cusco, Machu Picchu, Arequipa, Lake Titicaca, Huacachina, Amazonas.

## Landmarks & sights
- Machu Picchu (UNESCO)
- Rainbow Mountain (Vinicunca)
- Arequipa & Misti volcano
- Huacachina oasis

## Food & drink
- ceviche
- lomo saltado
- causa (stuffed potato)
- pisco sour
- ají de gallina

## Things to do
- Inca trail & Machu Picchu
- Andean treks (Rainbow Mountain)
- Lima's top restaurants
- camel rides (Cusco)
- desert oasis (Huacachina)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Cusco City Center Hotel, Lima Miraflores Hotel, Arequipa City Hotel.

## Good to know
- Dry season: May–September (best for Cusco)
- Altitude — spend 1–2 days acclimatizing in Cusco
