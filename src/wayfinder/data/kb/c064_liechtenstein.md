## Overview
One of the world's smallest countries, tucked between Switzerland and Austria: the castle of Vaduz, the high ski village of Malbun, and quiet Alpine valleys.

## Where to go
Top places: Vaduz, Malbun, Schaan, Rhine valley.

## Landmarks & sights
- Vaduz castle
- the Rhine valley views

## Food & drink
- Alpine cheese
- game (wild boar)
- local wine (Scheiben)

## Things to do
- skiing (Malbun, Tschaggers)
- hiking the Malbun plateau
- cycling the Rhine valley

## Where to stay

Stay options: Vaduz City Hotel, Malbun Alpine Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer: hiking; winter: skiing (small but excellent)
- It's tiny — a day trip from Switzerland or Austria works
