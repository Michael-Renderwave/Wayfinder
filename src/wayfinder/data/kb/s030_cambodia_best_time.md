## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** January, February, December — best overall: **January**.
- **Shoulder:** March, April, November.
- **Off-season:** September, October, July, August, June, May.
- Climate reference point: **Phnom Penh** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Cambodia is tropical and its climate dominated by monsoons, so season are wet or dry, rather than the four seasons of cooler regions of the world.
- Nov-Mar is relatively windy and cool and is the most popular time to visit.
- Apr-May is hot and dry, and temperatures may peak at 40°C.
