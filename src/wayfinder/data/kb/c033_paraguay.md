## Overview
The river country of South America: Asunción's plazas, Itaipú Dam, Jesuit missions, and a gateway to Iguazú Falls — low-key and affordable.

## Where to go
Top places: Asunción, Ciudad del Este, Encarnación, Itaipú.

## Landmarks & sights
- Itaipú Dam (world's largest hydroelectric)
- Jesuit reductions (Trinidad, UNESCO)
- Iguazú Falls (border)

## Food & drink
- chipa (cheese bread)
- locro (corn stew)
- asado
- tereré (iced yerba-mate)
- empanadas

## Things to do
- Itaipú Dam tour
- Jesuit heritage (Trinidad)
- river life on the Paraná

## Where to stay

Stay options: Asunción City Hotel, Encarnación Riverfront Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer: December–February
- A quiet, off-the-beaten-path South American country
