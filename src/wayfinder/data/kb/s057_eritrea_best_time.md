## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** September, December, June — best overall: **September**.
- **Shoulder:** January, February, March.
- **Off-season:** May, October, April, November, July, August.
- Climate reference point: **Asmara** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Hot, dry desert strip along Red Sea coast; cooler and wetter in the central highlands (up to 610 mm (24 in) of rainfall annually); semiarid in western hills and lowlands; rainfall heaviest during June-September except in the coastal desert.
