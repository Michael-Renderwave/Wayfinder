## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** February, June, August — best overall: **February**.
- **Shoulder:** January, November, March.
- **Off-season:** October, December, September, July, May, April.
- Climate reference point: **Malé** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- The Maldives are tropical, with plenty of sunshine and temperatures around 26.4°C (79.5°F) to 31.5°C (88.7°F) throughout the year.
- However, rainfall increases considerably during the April-October southwest monsoon, particularly from June to November.
