## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** February, January, March — best overall: **February**.
- **Shoulder:** December, November, April.
- **Off-season:** May, June, August, September, October, July.
- Climate reference point: **Santo Domingo** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Tropical maritime with little seasonal temperature variation.
- There is a seasonal variation in rainfall.
- The island lies in the middle of the hurricane belt and is subject to severe storms from June to October.
- It experiences occasional flooding and periodic droughts.
