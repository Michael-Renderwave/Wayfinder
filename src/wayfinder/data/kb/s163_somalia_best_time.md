## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** January, February, December — best overall: **January**.
- **Shoulder:** March, September, June.
- **Off-season:** October, July, August, May, November, April.
- Climate reference point: **Mogadishu** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Major climatic factors are a year-round hot climate, seasonal monsoon winds, and irregular rainfall with recurring droughts.
- The southwest monsoon, a sea breeze, makes the period from about May to October the mildest season at Mogadishu.
- The December-February period of the northeast monsoon is also relatively mild, although prevailing climatic conditions in Mogadishu are rarely pleasant.
- The "tangambili" periods that intervene between the two monsoons (October–November and March–May) are hot and humid.
