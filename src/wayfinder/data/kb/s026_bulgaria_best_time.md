## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Interior: Continental in most of the interior: moderately cold winters with occasional heavy snowfalls; hot and dry or mildly humid summers.
- Winter: The temperatures during the winter period average between -5 and 0 in the plains, between -2 and 3 at the seaside, and between -10 and -6 in the mountains.
- Summer: In the summer, the temperatures vary from 25 to 30 in the plains, from 21 to 28 on the coast of the Black Sea, and from 18 to 21 in the mountains.
