The Cayman Islands are a British Overseas Territory in the Caribbean with three islands: Grand Cayman, Cayman Brac, and Little Cayman. Grand Cayman's capital is George Town, home to the Queen Elizabeth II Botanic Park and a walkable downtown core.

Best time to visit is December through April. June through November is hurricane season, and the water is warmest (80–84°F). Stingray City, a snorkeling stop on the north shore, is open daily and costs about $40 per person.

The local currency is the Cayman dollar (KYD), fixed at a 1:1 exchange with the US dollar. Taxis are metered and widely available; a ride from Owen Roberts International Airport to George Town costs roughly $20–$30. Public buses (the B10 and B11) run along the main east-west road and cost $1–$3.
