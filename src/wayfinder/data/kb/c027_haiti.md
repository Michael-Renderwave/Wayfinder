## Overview
The Caribbean's most storied nation: the first Black republic, the Citadelle fortress, and a living art, music, and spiritual tradition centered on Jacmel and Port-au-Prince.

## Where to go
Top places: Port-au-Prince, Jacmel, Les Cayes, Île de la Tortue.

## Landmarks & sights
- Jacmel old town
- Môle-Saint-Nicolas

## Food & drink
- griot (fried pork)
- legim (bean & pea stew)
- soup joumou (independence soup)
- fresh fish & tropical fruit

## Things to do
- colonial-era history (Citadelle)
- coastal fishing villages (Jacmel)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Port-au-Prince City Hotel, Jacmel Coastal Hotel.

## Good to know
- Check current travel advisories before booking
- Rich culture — approach with respect and local guides
