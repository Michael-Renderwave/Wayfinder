## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** May, September, August — best overall: **May**.
- **Shoulder:** June, July, October.
- **Off-season:** April, January, November, February, March, December.
- Climate reference point: **Roseau** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Tropical; moderated by northeast trade winds; heavy rainfall.
- Flash floods are a constant threat; destructive hurricanes can be expected during the late summer months and well into the fall.
