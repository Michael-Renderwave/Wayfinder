## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** January, June, July — best overall: **January**.
- **Shoulder:** September, October, December.
- **Off-season:** February, May, April, August, November, March.
- Climate reference point: **Buenos Aires** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Spring and fall often exhibit rapid temperature reversals; several days of extremely hot weather may be followed by several days of cold weather, then back to extremely hot.
- Northwest Argentinas climate varies by altitude with lowland areas experiencing hot summers and mild winters while icy conditions prevail at the highest altitudes.
- The rainfall changes a lot within a small distance ranging from more than 1,000 mm (39 in) to just under 200 (8 in) less than 100 km (62 mi) away to the east.
- One defining characteristic of the climate is the strong, persistent winds that blow across the region, making the temperature feel much colder than it is.
