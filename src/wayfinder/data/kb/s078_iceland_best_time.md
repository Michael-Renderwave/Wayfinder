## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** July, August, June — best overall: **July**.
- **Shoulder:** September, May, April.
- **Off-season:** October, November, March, February, December, January.
- Climate reference point: **Reykjavik** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Despite its name, Iceland has mild winters for a country at its latitude – owing to the warming effect of the Atlantic Gulf Stream – especially in comparison with the Russian climate.
- Coastal, south-western parts of Iceland enjoy a maritime temperate climate; its winters are often compared to those of the Pacific Northwest in the U.S.A., although the winter winds can be bitter.
- However, Icelands rapidly changing weather has given rise to the local saying: If you dont like the weather, wait five minutes! The summers are usually cool and more temperate than elsewhere at the same latitude (the effect of the ocean again), averaging at 12°C (54°F).
