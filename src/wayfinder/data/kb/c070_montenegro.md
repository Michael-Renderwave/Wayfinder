## Overview
A small Adriatic country of the deep Bay of Kotor, the Durmitor's high peaks and skiing, and the old towns of Kotor, Perast, and Budva — a compact, scenic, affordable base.

## Where to go
Top places: Podgorica, Kotor, Budva, Sveti Stefan, Durmitor National Park, Perast.

## Landmarks & sights
- the Bay of Kotor (UNESCO)
- the Durmitor National Park (UNESCO)
- Cetinje

## Food & drink
- black risotto (Kotor)
- prosciutto (prosciutto)
- fresh seafood
- rakia

## Things to do
- kayaking (the Bay of Kotor)
- skiing (the Durmitor)
- beaches (Budva)
- hiking (the Durmitor)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Kotor Old Town Hotel, Budva Beachfront Hotel, Zabljak Mountain Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer: the beaches; winter: the skiing
- The Bay of Kotor is the highlight
