## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** June, August, September — best overall: **June**.
- **Shoulder:** May, March, April.
- **Off-season:** July, October, December, February, November, January.
- Climate reference point: **Amsterdam** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Every month of the year has rainfall, with no wet or dry season.
- The best time to go is from May to September (daily maximum 18-19 up to 23 , but April and October can also see mild and sunny weather.
- In winter, temperatures are around 0-6 most of the time, although frosty periods occur each winter, generally down to -5 , but frosts of -10 are common too.
- Severe winters offer many ice tours, with the famous Frisian Elfstedentocht (eleven town tour or eleven cities tour) being by far the most popular event.
