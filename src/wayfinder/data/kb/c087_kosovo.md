## Overview
A small, young country of the old towns of the Prizren and the Gjakova, the quiet lakes of the Plav, and the hills and castles of the interior — a scenic, affordable, food-rich base.

## Where to go
Top places: Pristina, Prizren, Gjakova, Dukagjini highlands, Uroševac.

## Landmarks & sights
- Prizren's old town
- Gjakova's old town
- the Dukagjini highlands
- Uroševac (the old fortress)

## Food & drink
- the burek
- the ćevapi
- the local coffee
- the local wine (the hills)

## Things to do
- the Prizren (the old town)
- the Gjakova (the old town)
- the Plav (the lakes)
- the castles (the hills)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Pristina City Center Hotel, Prizren Old Town Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer: the old towns; the high season is June–August
- The old towns are the highlight — walk them
