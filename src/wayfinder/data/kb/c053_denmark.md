## Overview
Scandinavia's compact classic: Copenhagen's Nyhavn canals, New Nordic food, Tivoli, and the quiet Danish islands — Bornholm, Fanø, and the long, flat beaches of the west coast.

## Where to go
Top places: Copenhagen, Aarhus, Odense, Bornholm, Wadden coast.

## Landmarks & sights
- Little Mermaid (Copenhagen)
- Nyhavn
- Tivoli Gardens
- the Ribe Viking Museum

## Food & drink
- smørrebrød (open-faced rye)
- New Nordic tasting menus
- herring & pickles
- Danish pastries (kanelbolde)

## Things to do
- cycling everywhere
- Bornholm's beaches & granite
- stargazing on Fanø (dark-sky island)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Copenhagen City Center Hotel, Copenhagen Airport Hotel, Aarhus Riverside Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer: June–August
- Cycling is the local way to move — rent one in Copenhagen
