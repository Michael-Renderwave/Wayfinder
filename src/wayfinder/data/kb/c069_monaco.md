## Overview
The world's most densely populated country: Monaco-Ville's old town, Monte Carlo's casino and yachts, and the Oceanographic Museum — a tiny, glamorous Mediterranean state.

## Where to go
Top places: Monaco, Monte Carlo, Monaco-Ville, Larvotto, Menton.

## Landmarks & sights
- the Monaco Casino
- the Prince's Palace
- the Oceanographic Museum
- the harbor

## Food & drink
- French Mediterranean (seafood)
- fine dining (three Michelin stars)
- pastel (local pastry)

## Things to do
- the yachts (the port)
- the casino
- the beach (the Larvotto)
- the Formula 1 (May)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Monte Carlo Bay Hotel, Monaco Old Town Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer: the yachts; the high season is July–August
- Parking is hard — the train from Nice is the easiest way in
