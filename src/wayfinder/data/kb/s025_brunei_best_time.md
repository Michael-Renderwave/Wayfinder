## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** July, September, June — best overall: **July**.
- **Shoulder:** October, February, March.
- **Off-season:** May, August, November, January, April, December.
- Climate reference point: **Bandar Seri Begawan** (2023-2025 normals).
