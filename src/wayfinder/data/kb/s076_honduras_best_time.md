## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** February, March, January — best overall: **February**.
- **Shoulder:** April, December, May.
- **Off-season:** November, July, August, September, June, October.
- Climate reference point: **Tegucigalpa** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- The average high temperature nationwide is 32°C (90°F) and the average low is 20°C (68°F).
- The Caribbean coast can experience a lot of rain, the heaviest being from September to February.
- In Tegucigalpa, the capital, the climate remains more temperate and the dry season takes place from December to May.
- The capital can get chilly between December and January when the temperature in the city hovers around 23°C (73°F).
