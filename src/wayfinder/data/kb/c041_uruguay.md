## Overview
The quiet corner of South America: Montevideo's plazas, Punta del Este's beaches, colonial Colonia, and a strong mate-and-asado culture.

## Where to go
Top places: Montevideo, Punta del Este, Colonia del Sacramento, Punta del Diablo.

## Landmarks & sights
- Punta del Este beaches
- Rambla de Montevideo (seaside boardwalk)

## Food & drink
- asado (Uruguayan BBQ)
- chivito (sandwich)
- mate tea
- fresh seafood & Tannat wine

## Things to do
- beach days (Punta del Este)
- vineyards (Canelones)
- colonial Colonia
- tango & mate culture

## Where to stay
- Punta del Este (resorts)
Stay options: Montevideo City Hotel, Punta del Este Beach Resort.

## Good to know
- Summer: December–March (southern hemisphere)
- Safe, quiet, and affordable by Caribbean standards
