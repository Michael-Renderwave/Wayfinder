## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** August, June, July — best overall: **August**.
- **Shoulder:** September, April, November.
- **Off-season:** March, October, January, May, December, February.
- Climate reference point: **Valletta** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Maltas Climate and geography is influenced by the Mediterranean Sea and is similar to other Mediterranean climates.
- Summers are virtually guaranteed to be dry and hot.
- Temperatures range from a low of approximately 15 in December, January, February and March to highs of approximately 29 in June, July, August and September.
