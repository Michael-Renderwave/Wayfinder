## Overview
Baltic's digital frontier and old-town gem: Tallinn's Hanseatic walls, the quiet island of Saaremaa, and long, sandy, wind-blown Baltic beaches.

## Where to go
Top places: Tallinn, Saaremaa, Pärnu, Lahemaa.

## Landmarks & sights
- Tallinn old town (UNESCO)
- Toompea castle
- Kuressaare castle (Saaremaa)

## Food & drink
- herring & potato salad
- porridge (kama)
- blueberry pie (mustamajad)
- rye bread

## Things to do
- coastal cycling (Saaremaa)
- stargazing (dark-sky islands)
- Pärnu beach days

## Where to stay
- Tallinn (old town)
Stay options: Tallinn Old Town Hotel, Kuressaare Castle Hotel.

## Good to know
- Shoulder season (May, September) is quiet and lovely
- The old town is compact — walk it in a day
