## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** September, July, August — best overall: **September**.
- **Shoulder:** June, October, May.
- **Off-season:** November, April, December, March, January, February.
- Climate reference point: **St. John's** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- The climate is tropical marine with little seasonal temperature variation.
- The islands experience hurricanes and tropical storms (July to October), and periodic droughts.
