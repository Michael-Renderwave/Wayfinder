The most distinctive Cayman gifts are ceramics from 3 Girls & A Kiln (their cherry and sea-turtle pieces are the best sellers), conch-shell jewelry, and local rum — Cayman's "Grog" rum is the local favorite at $25–$40 a bottle. Local chocolatiers on South Church Street sell cinnamon and sea-salt dark chocolate bars for $8–$12.

If you are buying for a family, a practical gift basket runs $100–$180: rum, rum cakes from the Queen's Royal Exchange, and a small ceramic piece from 3 Girls & A Kiln. Most shops gift-wrap for free and will pack ceramics in bubble wrap for air travel — ask for the "fly-safe" box, which the staff will assemble for a few dollars.
