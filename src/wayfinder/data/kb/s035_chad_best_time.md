## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** January, February, December — best overall: **January**.
- **Shoulder:** November, October, March.
- **Off-season:** April, May, June, September, August, July.
- Climate reference point: **N'Djamena** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Each year a tropical weather system known as the inter-tropical front crosses Chad from south to north, bringing a wet season that lasts from May to October in the south, and from June to September in the Sahel.
