## Overview
A land of lakes, forests, and design: Helsinki's white cathedral, the midnight sun of Lapland, the northern lights, and the sauna — the national pastime.

## Where to go
Top places: Helsinki, Lapland, Åland islands, Tampere, thousand lakes.

## Landmarks & sights
- Helsinki Cathedral (white)
- Kakslauttanen glass igloos (Lapland)
- Lake Saimaa

## Food & drink
- karitaima (fish soup)
- porridge
- reindeer
- Finnish sauna & bread

## Things to do
- northern lights (Sept–March)
- the midnight sun (June–July)
- Lapland hiking & husky safaris
- sauna & swim (everywhere)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Helsinki City Center Hotel, Helsinki Airport Hotel, Rovaniemi Arctic Hotel.

## Good to know
- Northern lights: September–March, away from city lights
- Lapland is best December–March for the snow
