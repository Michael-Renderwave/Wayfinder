## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** December, November, July — best overall: **December**.
- **Shoulder:** January, February, October.
- **Off-season:** April, May, June, September, August, March.
- Climate reference point: **Tokyo** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- The temperatures are warm but not hot, theres not too much rain, and March–April brings the justly famous cherry blossoms (sakura) and is a time of revelry and festivals.
- Summer starts with a dreary rainy season (known as tsuyu or baiu) in June and turns into a steam bath in July–August, with extreme humidity and the temperature heading as high as 40 °C.
- The upside, though, is a slew of fireworks shows (花火大会 hanabi taikai) and festivals big and small.
- Temperatures and humidity become more tolerable, fair days are common and fall colors can be just as impressive as cherry blossoms.
