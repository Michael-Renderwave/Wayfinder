## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** June, August, May — best overall: **June**.
- **Shoulder:** April, October, July.
- **Off-season:** September, March, December, February, November, January.
- Climate reference point: **London** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- British rain is world-renowned, but in practice, it rarely rains more than two or three hours at a time and often parts of the country stay dry for many weeks at a time, especially in the East.
- Spring and autumn often show the greatest regional disparity of temperature with single figures in the north versus mid-20s in the south.
- Wales and Northern Ireland tend to experience cool to mild temperatures and moderate rainfall, while the hills of Wales occasionally experience heavy snowfall.
- The UK lies between 50 and 60 degrees north of the equator so the sun sets in Plymouth at 16:16 and in Inverness at 15:32 on the winter solstice.
