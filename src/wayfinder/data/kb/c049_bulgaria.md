## Overview
A underrated Balkan mix: Plovdiv's 2,000-year-old theater, Rila Monastery's painted halls, Black Sea beaches, and two great Alpine-style mountain parks.

## Where to go
Top places: Sofia, Plovdiv, Varna, Black Sea coast, Rila, Pirin mountains.

## Landmarks & sights
- Ancient Theatre of Plovdiv (UNESCO)
- Rila Monastery (UNESCO)
- Boyana Chapel (Sofia, UNESCO)
- Balchik Botanical Garden

## Food & drink
- sarmi (vine-leaf rolls)
- banitsa (cheese pastry)
- shop salad
- rakia

## Things to do
- Rila & Pirin mountain hiking
- skiing (Bansko, Borovets)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Sofia City Center Hotel, Plovdiv Old Town Hotel, Varna Beach Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best: June–September
- Plovdiv is one of Europe's oldest cities — the old town is a UNESCO site
