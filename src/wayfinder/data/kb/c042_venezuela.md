## Overview
A country of superlatives: the world's highest waterfall (Ángel Falls), the Los Roques archipelago, and Andean peaks — wild, remote, and beautiful.

## Where to go
Top places: Caracas, Los Roques, Canaima, Mérida, Margarita Island.

## Landmarks & sights
- Ángel Falls (world's highest waterfall)
- Los Roques archipelago
- Gran Sabana tepuis

## Food & drink
- arepas
- pabellón criollo
- cachapas (sweet corn cakes)
- empanadas & fresh fruit

## Things to do
- Ángel Falls trek (multi-day)
- beach days (Margarita)
- Andes (Mérida)
- rainforest (Gran Sabana)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Caracas City Hotel, Mérida Andes Hotel.

## Good to know
- Check current travel advisories before booking
- Dramatic landscapes — go with local guides
