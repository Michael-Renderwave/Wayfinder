## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** June, July, August — best overall: **June**.
- **Shoulder:** September, October, May.
- **Off-season:** April, November, December, March, February, January.
- Climate reference point: **San Jose** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- The year can be split into two periods, the dry season known to the residents as summer, and the rainy season, known locally as winter.
- The summer, or dry season, lasts from December to April, and winter, or rainy season, lasts from May to November.
- The winter, or rainy season, coincides closely with the eastern Pacific and Atlantic/Caribbean Cyclones seasons.
- Despite being at tropical latitudes, Costa Rica is rarely struck by hurricanes, with the three most recent notable occurrences being en:w:Hurricane Nate (2017), en:w:Hurricane Otto, and en:w:Hurricane Cesar–Douglas in 2017, 2016, and 1996, respectively.
