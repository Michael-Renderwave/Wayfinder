## Overview
A Maya heartland: Tikal's giant pyramids, Antigua's colonial plazas, Lake Atitlán's ring of villages, and some of the region's best food and markets.

## Where to go
Top places: Guatemala City, Antigua, Lake Atitlán, Tikal, Quetzaltenango, Flores.

## Landmarks & sights
- Tikal Maya pyramids (UNESCO)
- Antigua cobblestone center (UNESCO)
- Lake Atitlán
- Sumidero Canyon

## Food & drink
- pepián (sauce with rice)
- kakaw (cacao drink)
- pan de elote
- fresh fruit & Guatemalan coffee

## Things to do
- Tikal sunrise pyramid visits
- jungle treks (Acatenango volcano)
- weaving markets (Chichicastenango)
- kayaking Lake Atitlán

## Where to stay

Stay options: Antigua City Center Hotel, Lake Atitlán Hotel, Tikal Gateway Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best: December–April (dry)
- Carry small bills for markets and cooperatives
