## Overview
A small Balkan country of the big capital of Belgrade, the Danube river, the Fruška Gora mountain and wine, and the castles of the Smederevo — a scenic, affordable, food-rich base.

## Where to go
Top places: Belgrade, Danube, Fruška Gora, Sremska Karlovca, castles.

## Landmarks & sights
- Belgrade Fortress
- Fruška Gora (national park)
- Smederevo castle
- the Danube

## Food & drink
- goulash
- karađorđeva šnicla (the national schnitzel)
- rakija
- fresh bread

## Things to do
- the Belgrade Fortress
- the Danube (the river)
- the Fruška Gora (the mountain)
- the wine (the Fruška Gora)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Belgrade City Center Hotel, Fruška Gora Mountain Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer: the Danube; the high season is June–August
- The fortress is the highlight
