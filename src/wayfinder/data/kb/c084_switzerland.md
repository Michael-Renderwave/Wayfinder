## Overview
The Alpine country of the Matterhorn, the Lucerne's chapel bridge, the quiet, clean center of the Zurich, and the lakes of the Geneva — a scenic, expensive, unforgettable base.

## Where to go
Top places: Bern, Zurich, Geneva, Alps, Lucerne, Basel, lakes.

## Landmarks & sights
- the Matterhorn (Zermatt)
- the Chapel Bridge (Lucerne)
- the old town of Bern
- the lakes (Lugano, Geneva)

## Food & drink
- fondue
- rösti
- Swiss chocolate
- local cheese (Emmentaler, Gruyère)

## Things to do
- the Alps (Interlaken, Zermatt)
- Lucerne's Chapel Bridge
- the lakes (Lugano, Geneva)
- Bern's old town

## Where to stay

Stay options: Zurich City Center Hotel, Interlaken Alpine Hotel, Zermatt Matterhorn Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer: the Alps; the high season is June–August
- The trains are the best way to move — book the Swiss Travel Pass
