## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** June, August, July — best overall: **June**.
- **Shoulder:** May, September, April.
- **Off-season:** October, January, November, March, February, December.
- Climate reference point: **Mbabane** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Generally speaking, rain falls mostly during the summer months, often in the form of thunderstorms.
- Annual rainfall is highest on the Highveld in the west, between 1,000 and 2,000 mm depending on the year.
- The Highveld temperature is temperate and seldom uncomfortably hot while the Lowveld may record temperatures around 40 degrees Celsius in summer.
- Pack rain gear for the wet season, including shoes that can handle mud, and lightweight clothes such as shorts for the summer.
