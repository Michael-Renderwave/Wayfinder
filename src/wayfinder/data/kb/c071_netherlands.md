## Overview
The flat, canal-dotted, windmill country: Amsterdam's canals and museums, the quiet town of Delft, the Zaanse Schans windmills, and the long, sandy beaches of the Wadden Sea.

## Where to go
Top places: Amsterdam, Utrecht, Delft, Zaanse Schans, Veluwe, Wadden Sea.

## Landmarks & sights
- Amsterdam's canals (UNESCO)
- the Zaanse Schans (windmills)
- the Rijksmuseum
- Delft's old town

## Food & drink
- bitterballen
- herring
- Dutch cheese
- stroopwafels

## Things to do
- cycling (Amsterdam & the countryside)
- cheese (the Gouda market)
- windmills (the Zaanse Schans)
- beaches (the Wadden)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Amsterdam City Center Hotel, Amsterdam Airport Hotel, Utrecht Riverside Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer: June–August
- Cycling is the local way — rent one in Amsterdam
