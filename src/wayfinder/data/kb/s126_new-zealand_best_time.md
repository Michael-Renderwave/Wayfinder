## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** March, November, February — best overall: **March**.
- **Shoulder:** January, December, October.
- **Off-season:** September, August, May, April, June, July.
- Climate reference point: **Wellington** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- This effect is most pronounced in the South Island with the Southern Alps: the West Coast (New Zealand) receives 2000–7000 mm of rain per year, while coastal Canterbury (New Zealand) and Otago in the east receive just 500–800 mm.
- The warmest temperatures are generally found in the north and east of both islands, while the coolest temperatures are generally found in inland parts of both islands and the southern South Island.
- During summer solstice (around 21 December), sunrise is typically between 5:35 AM and 6:05 AM, and sunset between 8:35 PM and 9:00 PM in the North Island and 9:00 PM and 9:45PM in the South Island.
- During winter solstice (around 21 June), sunrise is typically between 7:25 AM and 7:50 AM in the North Island and between 7:50 AM and 8:30 AM in the South Island, and sunset is typically between 4:50 PM and 5:20 PM in both islands.
