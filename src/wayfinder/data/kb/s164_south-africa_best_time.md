## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** July, August, June — best overall: **July**.
- **Shoulder:** May, September, March.
- **Off-season:** October, April, January, November, December, February.
- Climate reference point: **Pretoria** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- The climate in South Africa ranges from desert and semi-desert in the north west of the country to sub-tropical on the eastern coastline.
- The rainy season for most of the country is in the summer, except in the Western Cape where the rains come in the winter.
- Winter temperatures hover around zero, summers can be very hot, in excess of 35 °C (95 °F) in some places.
