## Overview
A twin-island Caribbean getaway: 365 named beaches on Antigua, the quiet, undeveloped island of Barbuda, and English Harbour's historic naval dockyard.

## Where to go
Top places: St. John's, Barbuda, English Harbour, Falmouth.

## Landmarks & sights
- Shoal Bay
- Red Bay

## Food & drink
- grilled lobster & fresh fish
- Antigua pepper sauce

## Things to do
- '365 beaches' island hopping
- sailing & snorkeling
- kayaking around English Harbour

## Where to stay
- south-coast beachfront resorts (Antigua)
Stay options: St. John's City Hotel, Falmouth Beachfront Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best: November–April (dry trade-wind season)
- Ferry to Barbuda runs weather permitting — book ahead
