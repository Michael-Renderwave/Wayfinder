## Overview
A small Mediterranean archipelago of megalithic temples, Valletta's grand harbor, Mdina's quiet old town, and the turquoise Blue Lagoon of Comino.

## Where to go
Top places: Valletta, Sliema, Blue Lagoon, Mdina, Gozo.

## Landmarks & sights
- the megalithic temples (Ggantija, UNESCO)
- Valletta's harbor (UNESCO)
- Mdina (the 'Silent City')
- the Blue Lagoon

## Food & drink
- pastizzi (cheese/pea pastry)
- rabbit (fenkata)
- fresh seafood
- Gbejna cheese

## Things to do
- Gozo by boat
- diving (wrecks)
- beaches (Comino)
- Mdina's old town

## Where to stay

Stay options: Sliema Seaside Hotel, Valletta Old Town Hotel, Gozo Bay Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer is hot — the Blue Lagoon is crowded in July
- Rent a car or use the bus — the islands are small
