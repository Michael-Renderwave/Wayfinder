## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Much of Malawi is plateau, often reaching to 1,000 m (3,000 ft), and the temperature in these highlands is moderate, with the hottest period occurring during the autumn rainy season and the coolest and chilliest in winter.
- The hottest region in the country is the lower Shire River Valley well south of Blantyre.
- Temperatures along scenic Lake Malawi are generally warm, but with a cooling breeze, especially in the evenings.
- The rainy season begins in mid-October to early November and generally runs until March.
