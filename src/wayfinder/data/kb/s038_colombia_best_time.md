## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** September, July, August — best overall: **September**.
- **Shoulder:** January, June, October.
- **Off-season:** December, May, February, April, November, March.
- Climate reference point: **Bogotá** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Colombia is an equatorial country with amazing variance in altitude, so its going to be whatever temperature you like best all year long somewhere! Lacking the usual seasons, Colombians normally refer to rainy seasons as winter—but the differences in terrain and altitude mean the rainy seasons are different in every corner of the country! The one downside to all this climactic diversity, though, is that youll have to bring a fair amount of different clothes if you plan to travel extensively.
- Cities in the center like Bogotá and those to the north in Boyacá can reach temperatures below 0°C, so bring a coat.
