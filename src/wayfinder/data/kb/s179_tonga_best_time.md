## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** March, April, June — best overall: **March**.
- **Shoulder:** July, August, September.
- **Off-season:** February, October, May, January, December, November.
- Climate reference point: **Toga Village** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Tonga has a tropical rainforest climate (Af) with a distinct warm period (December–April), during which the temperatures rise above 32 , and a cooler period (May–November), with temperatures rarely rising above 27.
- There is a significant difference between the southern and northern islands, the latter closer to the Equator.
- Temperatures of 15 °C (59 °F) or lower are usually measured in the dry season and are more frequent in southern Tonga.
- The tropical cyclone season runs from 1 November to 30 April.
