## Overview
Island life with a pulse: jerk food, reggae, Dunn's River Falls, seven-mile beaches, and a music-and-spirits culture that runs deep.

## Where to go
Top places: Kingston, Montego Bay, Ocho Rios, Negril, Port Antonio, Blue Mountains.

## Landmarks & sights
- Dunn's River Falls
- Blue Hole (Ocho Rios)
- Bob Marley Museum (Kingston)

## Food & drink
- jerk chicken & pork
- escovitch fish
- ackee & saltfish (national dish)

## Things to do
- waterfall climbs (Dunn's)
- river rafting (White River)
- beach days + rum tours
- reggae music & dance

## Where to stay
- Montego Bay (resorts)
Stay options: Montego Bay Beach Resort, Ocho Rios City Hotel, Negril Beachfront Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best: December–April (dry)
- Kingston traffic is heavy — use taxis or rideshares
