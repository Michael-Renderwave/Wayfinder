## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** December, June, November — best overall: **December**.
- **Shoulder:** February, January, July.
- **Off-season:** October, September, April, March, May, August.
- Climate reference point: **Sanaa** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Mostly desert; hot and humid along west coast; temperate in western mountains affected by seasonal monsoon; extraordinarily hot, dry, harsh desert in east.
- The weather can be chilly in areas where the elevation is high.
- Sanaa for example is at an elevation of over 2,195 m (7,200 ft).
- During the winter months, the temperatures can fall to freezing point during the night.
