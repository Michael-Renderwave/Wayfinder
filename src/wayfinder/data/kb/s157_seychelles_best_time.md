## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** July, August, October — best overall: **July**.
- **Shoulder:** June, November, February.
- **Off-season:** March, April, May, January, September, December.
- Climate reference point: **Victoria** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Seychelles is hot and humid, with an average yearly temperature of 84°F (29°C), and average sea temperature rarely dropping below 81°F (27°C).
- The cooler season in Seychelles is during the southeast monsoon season (May to September) and the warmer season is during the northwest monsoon (November to March).
- April and October are "changeover months" between the two monsoons, when the wind is variable.
- The northwest monsoon season tends to be warmer with more rain, while the southeast monsoon season is usually drier and cooler.
