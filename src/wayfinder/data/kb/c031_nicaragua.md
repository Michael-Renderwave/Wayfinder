## Overview
Central America's quiet giant: twin volcano Ometepe Island, colonial Granada, Pacific surf, and some of the region's best value.

## Where to go
Top places: Managua, León, Granada, Ometepe Island, San Juan del Sur, Masaya.

## Landmarks & sights
- Ometepe twin volcanoes (Consejo, Maderas)
- Granada colonial center
- San Juan del Sur beaches

## Food & drink
- gallo pinto
- vigorón (pork, yuca, cabbage)
- quesillo
- fresh seafood & local rum

## Things to do
- volcano hikes (Ometepe)
- surfing (San Juan del Sur)
- colonial towns (León, Granada)
- jungle tours (Corn Island)

## Where to stay

Stay options: San Juan del Sur Beach Hotel, Granada Colonial Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best: December–April (dry)
- One of Central America's cheapest — go slow
