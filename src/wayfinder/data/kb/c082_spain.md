## Overview
The sun-and-sea, tapas-and-castles country: Madrid's Prado, Barcelona's Sagrada Família, the Alhambra of Granada, the Costa del Sol beaches, and the Basque food of the north. Huge, diverse, and one of the best food cultures on Earth.

## Where to go
Top places: Madrid, Barcelona, Granada & Andalusia, Seville, Valencia, Costa del Sol, the Basque Country, the Balearics (Mallorca), Galicia.

## Landmarks & sights
- the Sagrada Família (Barcelona) — Gaudí's unfinished basilica; book entry + tower slots
- the Park Güell & Casa Batlló (Barcelona) — Gaudí's mosaic park and the "bone" façade
- La Rambla & Gothic Quarter (Barcelona) — the lively pedestrian strip and the medieval lanes
- the Alhambra (Granada, UNESCO) — the Nasrid palaces + the Generalife gardens; book weeks ahead
- the Alcazaba & the Albaicín (Granada) — the fortress and the whitewashed Moorish quarter
- the Prado & Reina Sofía (Madrid) — El Greco, Velázquez, Goya, Picasso's Guernica
- the Plaza Mayor & the Retiro (Madrid) — the grand square and the lakeside park
- the Mezquita (Córdoba) — the red-and-white double arches of the old mosque
- the Alcázar of Seville — Mudéjar palace with gardens; the birthplace of Columbus
- the Giralda & the Cathedral (Seville) — the bell tower and the world's largest Gothic church
- the Valencia Old Town & the City of Arts & Sciences — the medieval center + the futuristic complex
- the Basque old towns — San Sebastián, Bilbao, the "pintxo" streets
- the Costa del Sol & Costa Blanca beaches — Malaga, Nerja, Alicante
- the Picos de Europa — dramatic northern mountains, hiking & small villages
- the Rías Baixas (Galicia) — estuary coast, world-class oysters & green wine

## Food & drink
- tapas & pintxos — the art of small plates; the Basque "pintxo" bars are the best
- paella — the saffron rice; Valencian (chicken/duck) is the classic; go at lunch
- jamón ibérico — the acorn-fed cured ham; the "bellota" grade is the top
- churros — fried dough with thick chocolate; the Madrid morning ritual
- gazpacho — cold tomato soup; a summer staple in Andalusia
- tortilla española — the Spanish omelette; "con cebolla" or without
- cocido madrileño — the Madrid stew; a cold-weather comfort dish
- croquetas — the croquette, potato-bechamel; a bar snack
- montaditos — small bread bites with toppings; a bar-hopping staple
- the local wine — Rioja, Ribera del Duero, and the green Albariño of Galicia
- seafood — grilled sardines, the "pulpo a la gallega" (octopus) of Galicia
- the Basque txuleta — the thick dry-aged rib; a San Sebastián icon

## Things to do
- 2-day Madrid: Prado + Plaza Mayor + the Retiro + a tapas crawl in La Latina
- 2-day Barcelona: Sagrada Família + Park Güell + the Gothic Quarter + a beach
- the Andalusia loop: Seville → Córdoba → Granada (the Alhambra) → the white villages
- the Basque food crawl: pintxos in San Sebastián, a txuleta dinner, a txakoli tasting
- a Costa del Sol / Costa Blanca beach week: Malaga, Nerja, or Alicante
- a Galicia day trip: the Rías Baixas oysters + the green Albariño
- flamenco: a "tablao" show in Seville or Granada (book the "tablaos" ahead)
- bullfighting: the "Plaza de Toros" in Madrid or Seville (a cultural, not just a sport, thing)
- the "Picos de Europa" — a 2-day hike in the northern mountains
- the "Rías Baixas" — a day trip for oysters + the "Albariño" wine
- the "Basque" — a 2-day food crawl in San Sebastián + Bilbao
- a "Costa del Sol" beach week — Malaga, Nerja, or "Almería"

## Good to know
- Summer is hot in Andalusia (35°C+); the coast and the north are cooler
- The tapas are the highlight — go early, before the dinner rush (lunch ~14:00, dinner ~21:00)
- The "sobremesa" — the post-meal chat; don't rush the table
- Book the Alhambra & the Sagrada Família weeks ahead — they sell out
- The "siesta" (15:00–17:00) is real in smaller towns; shops close
- Tipping ~5–10% is polite but not expected — it's in the bill
- The "tapas" in Andalusia are often free with a drink — order the wine, get the plate
