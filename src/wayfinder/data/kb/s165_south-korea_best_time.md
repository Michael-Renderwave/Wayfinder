## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** April, October, March — best overall: **April**.
- **Shoulder:** November, May, June.
- **Off-season:** September, August, July, February, January, December.
- Climate reference point: **Seoul** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Summer starts with a dreary rainy season (장마철 jangma-cheol) in June and turns into a steambath in July–August, with extreme humidity and the temperature heading as high as 35 °C.
- Autumn, starting in September, is perhaps the best time to be in Korea.
- Temperatures and humidity become more tolerable, fair days are common and the justly renowned fall colors make their appearance.
- Winter is a good time to go skiing or hot-spring hopping, and the Korean invention of ondol (온돌, floor heating) helps defrost any parts that froze outside.
