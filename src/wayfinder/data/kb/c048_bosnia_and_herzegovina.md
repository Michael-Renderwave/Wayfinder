## Overview
The Balkans' most scenic mix: Mostar's rebuilt old bridge, the Blagaj dervish monastery, Sarajevo's bazaar-and-tunnel history, and some of Europe's best rafting canyons.

## Where to go
Top places: Sarajevo, Mostar, Blagaj, Jajce, Una valley.

## Landmarks & sights
- Stari Most (Old Bridge, UNESCO)
- Blagaj Tekke
- Yellow Fortress (Jajce)
- Baščaršija bazaar (Sarajevo)

## Food & drink
- ćevapi (the national dish)
- burek (cheese/meat pastry)
- Bosnian coffee
- somun bread

## Things to do
- rafting the Tara river
- the Una river canyon
- skiing (Bjelašnica)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Sarajevo City Center Hotel, Mostar Old Town Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best: June–September
- Carry cash — KMark cards are patchy in smaller towns
