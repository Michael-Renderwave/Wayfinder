## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** January, December, November — best overall: **January**.
- **Shoulder:** February, March, April.
- **Off-season:** May, October, June, August, September, July.
- Climate reference point: **Abuja** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Varies; equatorial in the south, tropical in the centre, arid in the north.
- Natural hazards include periodic droughts and flooding.
- Tornadoes and hurricanes are rare because they typically are weak at this stage and travel west of the Atlantic.
