## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** January, February, December — best overall: **January**.
- **Shoulder:** August, November, March.
- **Off-season:** April, July, September, June, October, May.
- Climate reference point: **Lomé** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- The climate is generally tropical with average temperatures ranging from 27°C on the coast to about 30°C in the northernmost regions, with a dry climate and characteristics of a tropical savanna.
- To the south there are two seasons of rain (the first between April and July and the second between October and November).
