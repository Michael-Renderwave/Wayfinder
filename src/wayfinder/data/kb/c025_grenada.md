## Overview
A quiet 'Spice Island' in the southern Caribbean: the twin volcanic spires of the Pitons, a working 18th-century fort in St. George's, and a world-class underwater sculpture park.

## Where to go
Top places: St. George's, Grand Anse, Carenage, Carriacou.

## Landmarks & sights
- Fort George (1756, still standing)
- Grand Etang (lagoon)

## Food & drink
- garlic shrimp (the national dish)
- crab & oyster dishes
- breadfruit & coconut cooking

## Things to do
- snorkeling the Underwater Sculpture Park
- diving & reef swims

## Where to stay
- Grand Anse (south-coast resorts)
Stay options: Grand Anse Beachfront Hotel, Carenage Harbour Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best: January–mid-June (driest)
- The 'Isle of Spice' — nutmeg grew here; the climate is mild year-round
