- **Shoulder:** June, September, April.
- **Off-season:** December, February, October, March, November, January.

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Temperatures in the north of Mainland Portugal are cooler than in the south, with snow on the highest mountains in the north such as Serra da Estrela.
- Madeira and the Azores have a narrower temperature range as expected given their insularity, with the former having low precipitation in most of the archipelago and the latter being wet and rainy.
- Spring and summer months are usually sunny and maximum temperatures are very high during July and August, averaging 35-40 in the interior of the country, 30-35 in the north.
- Portugals climate can be classified as Mediterranean (particularly the southern parts of the Algarve and Alentejo, though they are on Atlantic shore).
