## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Distribution and frequency of rainfall are unpredictable due to the continental climate of the country.
- Heavy storms are frequent after hot summer days, and so do more days long still rainfalls in the autumn.
- The western part of the country usually receives more rain than the eastern part, and severe droughts may occur in summertime.
- Weather conditions in the Great Plain can be especially harsh, with hot summers, cold winters, and scant rainfall.
