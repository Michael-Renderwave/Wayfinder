## Overview
Budapest's castle, baths, and ruin bars; the big blue lake of Balaton; the wine hills of Tokaj; and the quiet caves of Aggtelek — a compact, affordable, food-rich Central Europe.

## Where to go
Top places: Budapest, Lake Balaton, Aggtelek caves, Visegrád, Tokaj wine region.

## Landmarks & sights
- Szechenyi Bath
- the Danube & Chain Bridge
- Aggtelek caves (UNESCO)

## Food & drink
- goulash
- lángos (fried dough)
- paprikás csirke (paprika chicken)
- Tokaj wine

## Things to do
- thermal bathing (Szechenyi, Gellért)
- Lake Balaton cycling
- Tokaj wine tasting
- the ruin bars (Budapest)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Budapest City Center Hotel, Budapest Buda Hill Hotel, Lake Balaton Resort.

## Good to know
- Thermal baths are cheap and local — go early
- Summer is the high season for Balaton
