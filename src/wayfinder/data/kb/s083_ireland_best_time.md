## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** May, June, August — best overall: **May**.
- **Shoulder:** February, October, July.
- **Off-season:** September, November, December, January, March, April.
- Climate reference point: **Dublin** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- In Ireland you may indeed experience four seasons in one day, so pack accordingly and keep up-to-date with the latest weather forecast.
- You may notice slight differences in temperature between the north and south of the country, and more rain in the west compared with the east.
- Mean daily winter temperatures vary from 4 °C to 7 °C, and mean daily summer temperatures vary from 14.5 °C to 16 °C.
- Regardless of when you visit Ireland, even in the middle of the summer, you will more than likely experience rain, so if you intend being outdoors, a waterproof coat is recommended.
