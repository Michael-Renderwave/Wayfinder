George Town is the heart of Grand Cayman's souvenir scene. Now it's time to shop: you'll find a mix of tourist shops, local gift shops, and unique places throughout George Town to check out. Head north on Church Street and make your first stop at 3 Girls & A Kiln. The shop is well known for its cheery ceramics, including the famous "Cayman" mugs and painted sea-turtle pottery that pack safely for the flight home.

The Queen's Royal Exchange, just off Bath Street, stocks local rum, rum cakes, and handmade jewelry. The Craft Shop on South Church Street carries conch-shell crafts and island art. For beachwear and swim trunks, the West End Shopping Village has a larger selection and usually better prices than the downtown shops.

Most shops are open 9:00–18:00 Sunday through Thursday and close early on Friday afternoons. Prices are quoted in Cayman dollars but US dollars are accepted everywhere at 1:1. Budget roughly $80–$200 for a solid round of gift shopping per person.
