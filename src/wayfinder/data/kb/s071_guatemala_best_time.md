## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** January, March, February — best overall: **January**.
- **Shoulder:** December, April, November.
- **Off-season:** May, July, October, June, August, September.
- Climate reference point: **Guatemala City** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- In Puerto Barrios, on the Caribbean coast, average temperatures range from 20.1 °C (68.3 °F) in January to 29.5 °C in March, reaching 3,075 mm (121.1 inches) of rain per year.
- In the mountains, the climate is generally slightly cooler and tends to be less rainy, reaching just 802.1 mm (31.59 inches) in Quetzaltenango.
- In Guatemala City, the average maximum temperatures is 27 ºC (80.6 ºF) in the months of April and May and lows of 13.2 ºC (55.8 ºF) in January and 2.3 ºC (36.1 ºF) in Quetzaltenango.
- In the highlands, the rainy season runs from May to October.
