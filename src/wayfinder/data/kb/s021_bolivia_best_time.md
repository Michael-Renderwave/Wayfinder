## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** March, April, May — best overall: **March**.
- **Shoulder:** February, June, November.
- **Off-season:** January, December, July, October, September, August.
- Climate reference point: **La Paz** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Bolivias climate remains relatively similar from one climatic zone to another.
- In most parts of the country winters are dry and summers are somewhat wet.
- Despite its tropical latitude, the altitude of cities like La Paz keeps things cool, and warm clothing is advised during the months of April and May.
- April through October, the winter months, are typically colder and drier.
