## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** March, May, April — best overall: **March**.
- **Shoulder:** February, January, November.
- **Off-season:** October, December, July, August, September, June.
- Climate reference point: **Belmopan** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- The dry season typically lasts from February to May and then the rainy season typically lasts through to November.
- Hurricanes that bring coastal flooding, especially in the south, are prevalent from June to November.
