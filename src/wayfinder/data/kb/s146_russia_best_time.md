## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Summers blaze warm to hot across the continental interior, while winters envelop nearly the entire nation in lasting snow cover and persistent cold, save for the temperate North Caucasus in the far south.
- In the remote far northeast, subarctic conditions create the worlds coldest permanently inhabited places, including Yakutsk—the planets coldest major city.
- Across this immense territory, ancient permafrost lies frozen beneath nearly two-thirds of the nation, while above ground the landscape ranges from tundra to the sun-warmed shores of the Black Sea coast.
- Between these extremes lies the heart of Russia: the continental climate zone that encompasses European Russias cities, southern Siberias valleys, and the Far Easts wilderness.
