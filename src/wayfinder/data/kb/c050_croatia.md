## Overview
The Adriatic's showpiece: Dubrovnik's walled old town, Plitvice's turquoise lakes, Split's Roman palace, and a hundred islands of pine-scented beaches and stone villages.

## Where to go
Top places: Zagreb, Dubrovnik, Split, Zadar, Plitvice Lakes, Istria, Hvar, Korčula.

## Landmarks & sights
- Dubrovnik walls (UNESCO)
- Plitvice Lakes (16 cascades, UNESCO)
- Diocletian's Palace (Split)
- St. James's Cathedral (Zadar)

## Food & drink
- black risotto (Dubrovnik)
- peka (slow-baked dish)
- octopus & Adriatic seafood
- pljeskavica (meat cake)

## Things to do
- kayaking the Krka waterfalls
- island hopping (Hvar, Korčula)
- cycling around Plitvice
- Istrian wine & truffles

## Where to stay

Stay options: Dubrovnik Old Town Hotel, Split Riva Hotel, Zadar Beachfront Hotel.

## Good to know
- July–August is peak — book islands early
- Fast cat ferries connect the islands
