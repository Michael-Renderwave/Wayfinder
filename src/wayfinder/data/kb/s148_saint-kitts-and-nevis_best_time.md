## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** December, March, January — best overall: **December**.
- **Shoulder:** June, November, July.
- **Off-season:** May, September, February, April, August, October.
- Climate reference point: **Basseterre** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Tropical tempered by constant sea breezes; little seasonal temperature variation; rainy season (May to November).
