## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Temperatures typically range from 26–32°C, with only slight seasonal variation thanks to the moderating influence of the surrounding Pacific Ocean.
- The islands experience a wet season from November to April, when heavy showers, thunderstorms, and occasional tropical disturbances are more common.
- The dry season, from May to October, brings clearer skies, calmer seas, and slightly cooler nights, making it the most pleasant time for travel.
- High tides, king tides, and storm surges may cause temporary flooding in some areas, especially during the wet season.
