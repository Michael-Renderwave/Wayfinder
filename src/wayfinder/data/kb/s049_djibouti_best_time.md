## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** December, January, February — best overall: **December**.
- **Shoulder:** November, March, October.
- **Off-season:** April, May, September, June, July, August.
- Climate reference point: **Djibouti** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Djiboutis climate is very hot, humid and arid, especially in the summer.
- The summer heat is moderated, however, by a sustained breeze in the coastal city of Djibouti.
- From October to April, the temperature is cooler, with occasional rain.
- Cyclones from the Indian Ocean create heavy rains and flash flooding.
