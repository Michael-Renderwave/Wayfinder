## Overview
A jungle-meets-ocean country: Maya ruins, the world's most famous dive hole (the Great Blue Hole), barrier-reef islands, and a Maya-English culture.

## Where to go
Top places: Belmopan, San Ignacio, Caye Caulker, Ambergris Caye, Blue Hole.

## Landmarks & sights
- Great Blue Hole
- Xunantunich Maya ruins

## Food & drink
- Belizean taco (stuffed tortilla)
- banana bread & guava

## Things to do
- cave tubing (ATM)
- jungle zip-lining & sloth spotting
- Maya ruins at Xunantunich

## Where to stay

Stay options: San Ignacio Jungle Lodge, Caye Caulker Island Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best: December–April (dry)
- One of Central America's best-value nature destinations
