## Overview
700+ islands of bright turquoise water: Nassau's markets and casinos, Paradise Island resorts, and the Exumas' sandbars, reefs, and famous pigs.

## Where to go
Top places: Nassau, Paradise Island, Exumas, Andros, Harbour Island.

## Landmarks & sights
- Pink Sand Beach (Harbour Island)
- Pig Beach (Exumas)
- Queen Elizabeth II Beach Park

## Food & drink
- conch salad & cracked conch
- peas & rice
- fresh lobster & snapper

## Things to do
- swimming with pigs (Exumas)
- sandbar hopping
- snorkeling the reef
- wreck diving

## Where to stay
- Nassau & Paradise Island (resorts)
Stay options: Nassau City Hotel, Paradise Island Resort.

## Good to know
- Best: December–April
- Hurricane season runs June–November — check the season when booking
