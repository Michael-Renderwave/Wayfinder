## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** October, August, September — best overall: **October**.
- **Shoulder:** June, July, March.
- **Off-season:** April, May, February, January, November, December.
- Climate reference point: **Athens** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Sporadic rains do occur during the dry season, but they tend to be rare, quick showers.
- Mid-July to mid-August is the height of summer, and the midday sun tends to get very strong; during this time, most Greeks avoid heavy physical activity outdoors between 13:00 and 17:00.
- It is best advised to get in tune with the local way of life by waking up early, doing all sightseeing and errands in the cool morning hours, and then spending the afternoon in the relaxing shade or at the beach.
- As strong as the sun may get on a summer afternoon, the low levels of atmospheric humidity in most areas of the country prevent the air from trapping much heat, and temperatures tend to dip to very pleasant levels in the evenings.
