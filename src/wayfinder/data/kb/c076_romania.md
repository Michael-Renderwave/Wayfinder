## Overview
A varied country of the big capital of Bucharest, the castles and Saxon towns of the Transylvania, the Danube Delta, and the Carpathian Mountains — a scenic, affordable, food-rich base.

## Where to go
Top places: Bucharest, Transylvania, Danube Delta, Carpathians, Sibiu.

## Landmarks & sights
- Peleș Castle
- the Danube Delta (UNESCO)
- the Saxon fortified churches (UNESCO)

## Food & drink
- sarmale (cabbage rolls)
- mămăligă (corn porridge)
- ciorbă (soup)
- local wine & țuică

## Things to do
- the castles (Peleș)
- the Danube Delta (birds)
- the Transylvania (Sibiu, Brașov)
- the Carpathians (skiing)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Bucharest City Center Hotel, Brașov Transylvania Hotel, Sulina Danube Delta Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer: the Danube Delta; winter: the Carpathians
- The castles are the highlight
