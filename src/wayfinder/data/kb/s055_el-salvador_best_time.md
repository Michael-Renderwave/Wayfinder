## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** January, February, December — best overall: **January**.
- **Shoulder:** March, April, November.
- **Off-season:** July, May, August, October, June, September.
- Climate reference point: **San Salvador** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- El Salvador has a tropical climate with a rainy season from May to October and a dry season the rest of the year.
- Temperatures vary with altitude, and are usually stable throughout the year.
- The Pacific coast tends to have warm temperatures, while the mountainous areas are cooler.
- As a country that is located between the Atlantic Ocean and the Caribbean Sea – El Salvador is vulnerable to hurricanes.
