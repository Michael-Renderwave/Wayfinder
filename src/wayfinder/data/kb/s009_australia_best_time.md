## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** October, March, February — best overall: **October**.
- **Shoulder:** September, April, August.
- **Off-season:** January, May, December, November, June, July.
- Climate reference point: **Canberra** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- The daytime maximum temperatures in the tropical city of Darwin rarely drop below 30°C (86°F) even in winter, while night temperatures in winter usually hover around 15–20°C (60–70°F).
- Temperatures in high-altitude areas of some southern regions can drop below freezing in winter (and sometimes even in the summer) and the Snowy Mountains in the southeast experience metres of winter snow.
- The dry season is in winter in the tropics and in summer in the south beyond the Great Dividing Range.
- If you are from the Northern Hemisphere, note the implications on cardinal directions: the sun (and stars) move anti-clockwise over here, and its the northern slopes that get most sun.
