## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** January, December, February — best overall: **January**.
- **Shoulder:** November, June, April.
- **Off-season:** March, May, September, July, August, October.
- Climate reference point: **Juba** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- South Sudans climate is similar to an equatorial or tropical climate, and has a rainy season of high humidity and large amounts of rainfall followed by a drier season.
