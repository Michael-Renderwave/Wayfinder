## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** July, September, August — best overall: **July**.
- **Shoulder:** June, May, October.
- **Off-season:** April, November, December, January, February, March.
- Climate reference point: **Port Louis** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Tropical, modified by southeast trade winds; warm, dry winter (May to November); hot, wet, humid summer (November to May); Natural hazards : Tropical cyclones can occur between November and April, however are most likely from late December until March.
- Mauritius has only two seasons, winter and summer.
- Temperatures do not differ greatly over the seasons.
- The climate on the central plateau is cooler than on the coastal areas.
