## What I do have (Wikivoyage climate notes)
- Bangladesh has a sub-tropical monsoon climate.
- There are six seasons in a year: winter (Dec–Jan), spring (Feb–Mar), summer (Apr–May), monsoon (June–July), autumn (Aug–Sep) and late autumn (Oct–Nov).
- During the rainy season, even big cities such as Dhaka and Chittagong get submerged quickly by torrential rains.
- The best time to visit is October to April.
