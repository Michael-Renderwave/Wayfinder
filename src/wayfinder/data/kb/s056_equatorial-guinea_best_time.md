## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** December, January, February — best overall: **December**.
- **Shoulder:** November, April, March.
- **Off-season:** May, August, June, July, October, September.
- Climate reference point: **Malabo** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Equatorial Guinea has two distinctive and very pronounced seasons: rainy and dry seasons.
- On the island of Bioko, April to October are the wettest months of the year, and December to March are the driest.
- On the mainland, these seasons are largely reversed, with July-August dry and the rest of the year wet.
