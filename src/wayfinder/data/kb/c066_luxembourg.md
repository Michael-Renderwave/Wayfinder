## Overview
A small, green, castle-dotted country: Luxembourg City's Bock Casemates, the 'Little Switzerland' of the Mullerthalz, and the ruin of Vianden — a quiet, affordable Alpine-adjacent base.

## Where to go
Top places: Luxembourg City, Mullerthalz, Vianden, Clervaux.

## Landmarks & sights
- the Bock Casemates (UNESCO)
- Vianden castle (ruin)
- the Grand Ducal Palace

## Food & drink
- baeckeoffe (stew)
- eel
- local beer

## Things to do
- hiking the Mullerthalz
- castle hopping (Vianden)
- cycling the Moselle valley

## Where to stay

Stay options: Luxembourg City Center Hotel, Vianden Castle View Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer: hiking; the old town is compact
- A good day-trip base for the Ardennes (Belgium)
