## Overview
A Central American mix: the Bay Islands' reefs and beaches, Copán's Maya ruins, and Pico Bonito's rainforest — value and adventure.

## Where to go
Top places: Tegucigalpa, Roatán, La Ceiba, Copán, Bay Islands.

## Landmarks & sights
- Copán Maya ruins (UNESCO)
- Pico Bonito National Park
- Roatán's coral coast

## Food & drink
- baleadas (tortilla sandwich)
- fried fish & yuca
- tropical fruit
- Honduran coffee

## Things to do
- Roatán beach & reef days
- Copán Maya ruins
- jungle adventure (Pico Bonito)
- diving & snorkeling

## Where to stay

Stay options: Roatán Beach Hotel, La Ceiba City Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best: December–April (dry)
- Bay-Island ferries run from La Ceiba — check weather
