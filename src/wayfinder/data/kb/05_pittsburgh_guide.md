For a Kennywood trip, stay in the North Hills / West Mifflin corridor: it is 10–15 minutes from the park gate. Hilton Garden Inn Pittsburgh – North Hills ($195/night, 3.1 mi to Kennywood), Homewood Suites by Hilton Pittsburgh North Hills ($180/night, 2.4 mi, suites with kitchens), and Residence Inn Pittsburgh North Hills ($210/night, 2.9 mi) all park free. Downtown options (The Westin Pittsburgh, $240/night) are 25–35 minutes away but put you on the South Side and Strip District.

Dining near Kennywood: the North Shore has river-view restaurants (about $25–$55 per person); the Strip District in downtown is Pittsburgh's food hall district with 30+ vendors ($12–$30 per person). For a Pittsburgh classic, order a pierogi and a jumbo shrimp at a South Side bar.

Transit: rideshare or taxi from downtown to Kennywood runs $25–$40 and takes 15–25 minutes in normal traffic; the P3 bus line is a $2.75 alternative but involves a transfer. Driving is the norm — allow an extra 20 minutes on Saturday afternoons.
