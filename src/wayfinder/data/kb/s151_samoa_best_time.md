## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** August, September, June — best overall: **August**.
- **Shoulder:** July, April, October.
- **Off-season:** December, January, February, March, May, November.
- Climate reference point: **Apia** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- The climate is tropical with a rainy (and tropical cyclone) season from October to March and a dry season from May to October.
- The country has an average annual temperature of 26.5°C.
- This makes Samoa an ideal winter holiday destination for tourists living in the southern hemisphere.
