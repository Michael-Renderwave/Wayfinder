## Overview
A big Caribbean island: Punta Cana's resort coast, the oldest cathedral city in the Americas (Santo Domingo), and Samaná's whale-watching bays.

## Where to go
Top places: Santo Domingo, Punta Cana, Cap Cana, Samaná, Saona Island.

## Landmarks & sights
- Saona pink-sand cays
- Samaná Bay

## Food & drink
- sancocho
- mofongo (mashed plantain)
- fresh fish & shrimp
- local rum

## Things to do
- Punta Cana resort beaches
- Samaná whale watching (Dec–Mar)
- cave & waterfall tours
- Saona Island trips

## Where to stay
- Punta Cana (resorts)
Stay options: Punta Cana Beach Resort, Samaná Bay Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best: December–April
- Taxis are not metered — agree fares in advance
