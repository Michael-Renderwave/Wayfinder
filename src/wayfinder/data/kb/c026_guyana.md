## Overview
The only English-speaking country in South America: deep Amazonian rainforest, Kaieteur Falls (higher than Niagara), and Rupununi's wild plains.

## Where to go
Top places: Georgetown, Kaieteur Falls, Kaieteur National Park, Rupununi Savanna.

## Landmarks & sights
- Kaieteur National Park

## Food & drink
- one pot (stew)
- pepperpot
- ground provisions (cassava, yam)

## Things to do
- Kaieteur Falls trips
- Amazonian jungle treks
- Rupununi wild horses & wildlife
- birdwatching (500+ species)

## Where to stay
- Kaieteur lodges (jungle)
Stay options: Georgetown City Hotel, Kaieteur Jungle Lodge.

## Good to know
- Dry season: November–May
- A true off-the-beaten-path destination — few visitors, big nature
