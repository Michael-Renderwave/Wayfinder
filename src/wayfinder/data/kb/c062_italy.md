## Overview
Italy's impossible mix: Rome's ancient center, Florence's art, Venice's canals, the Amalfi Coast's cliffs, and Tuscany's wine and hill towns — a food-and-art trip in every region.

## Where to go
Top places: Rome, Florence, Venice, Amalfi Coast, Sicily, Tuscany, Lake Como.

## Landmarks & sights
- the Colosseum (Rome) — the 80,000-seat amphitheater; go at 8:30 am to beat the lines
- the Roman Forum & the Palatine Hill — the political heart of the ancient empire; 2 hr walk
- the Vatican Museums + St. Peter's Basilica — the Sistine Chapel ceiling; go on a weekday morning
- the Trevi Fountain — throw a coin (back hand, right arm); go at dawn
- the Trastevere district — cobblestone lanes, 12th-century churches, the best pizza in Rome
- the Pantheon — the 2,000-year-old unreinforced concrete dome; the Oculus is open to the sky
- the Spanish Steps — 135 steps; the view of Piazza di Spagna
- the Duomo (Cathedral of Santa Maria del Fiore, Florence) — Brunelleschi's dome; climb 463 steps
- the Uffizi Gallery (Florence) — the Botticelli, the Leonardo, the Caravaggio; 2–3 hr
- the Ponte Vecchio — the 16th-century bridge with the jewelry shops; the only bridge spared in WWII
- the Accademia (Florence) — the David (Michelangelo); 15 m of marble
- the Grand Canal (Venice) — take a vaporetto (water bus) at dusk; the Rialto Bridge
- the St. Mark's Basilica — the gold mosaics; the Doge's Palace; the Bridge of Sighs
- the Murano & Burano islands — glass-blowing (Murano), the colorful houses (Burano)
- the Amalfi Coast — Positano (the postcard), Ravello (the view), Amalfi (the town)
- the Path of the Gods (Sentiero degli Dei) — 8 km cliff walk from Bomerano to Nocelle
- the Cinque Terre — five fishing villages on cliffs; the 5-trail (4 hr) connects them
- the Dolomites — the jagged peaks of the eastern Alps; the Tre Cime di Lavaredo
- the Lake Como — Bellagio (the diamond), Varenna (the view), Como (the town)
- the Sicily coast — Taormina (the amphitheater), Cefalù (the Norman cathedral), Zingaro Nature Reserve
- the Pompeii ruins — the city frozen in 79 AD; the Forum, the Villa of Mysteries
- the Herculaneum — better preserved than Pompeii; the mosaic floors, the wooden furniture
- the Etruscan towns — Orvieto (the Cathedral), Volterra (the bronze), Cortona (the view)
- the San Gimignano — the "Medieval Manhattan"; the 14 towers
- the Assisi — the birthplace of St. Francis; the upper & lower basilicas

## Food & drink
- cacio e pepe — Rome's 3-ingredient pasta (pecorino, black pepper, pasta); at Roscioli or Trapizzino
- carbonara — the real one has no cream; at Armando al Panaro (Rome)
- the supplì — fried rice balls; at Supplì (Rome, Via dei Serpenti)
- the pizza — Naples: L'Antica Pizzeria da Michele (Margherita), Sorbillo (Marinara)
- the gelato — the real thing: the cream is thick, the flavor intense; at Girolamo (Rome), Venchi (Florence)
- the tiramisù — the real one has no cream cheese; at Le Maschere (Rome), Il Caffe (Venice)
- the aperitivo — the 6–8 pm ritual; Negroni or Spritz; the snacks (grissini, olives, cheese) are free
- the wine — Chianti (Tuscany), Barolo (Piedmont), Brunello di Montalcino, Prosecco (Veneto), Nero d'Avola (Sicily)
- the olio extravergine — the best in Tuscany, Umbria, Puglia; taste at a frantoio (olive mill)
- the truffle — Alba (Piedmont) in autumn; the tartufo bianco (white truffle) at a truffle farm
- the prosciutto — Prosciutto di Parma (Parma), Prosciutto di San Daniele (Friuli)
- the cheese — Parmigiano Reggiano (the real one), Pecorino (Sicily), Mozzarella di Bufala (Puglia)
- the street food — the arancini (Sicily), the supplì (Rome), the panzerotti (Naples)
- the espresso — 1 euro, 30 seconds, standing at the bar; the cappuccino is a morning drink only
- the focaccia — the thick, olive-oil-soaked bread; at any forno in Liguria
- the limoncello — made from the lemons of Amalfi; try at a family-run limonaia

## Things to do
- Rome: 3–4 days of the ancient center + Trastevere + the Vatican + a day trip to Pompeii or Tivoli
- Florence: 2 days of the Duomo + the Uffizi + the Ponte Vecchio + the David
- Venice: 2 days of the Grand Canal + the islands (Murano, Burano) + a vaporetto at dusk
- the Amalfi Coast: 2–3 days; Positano + Ravello + the Path of the Gods + a boat to Capri
- the Cinque Terre: 1–2 days; the 5-trail + the villages; the boat from La Spezia
- Tuscany: 2–3 days of the wine + the hill towns (Siena, San Gimignano, Montepulciano)
- Sicily: 3–4 days; Palermo + Cefalù + Taormina + the Zingaro Reserve + the beach at San Vito
- the Dolomites: 2–3 days of the Tre Cime + the Alpe di Siusi + the Lago di Braies
- the Lake Como: 1–2 days; Bellagio + Varenna + the boat to Como
- the Puglia coast: 2–3 days; the trulli (Alberobello), the baroque (Matera), the beach (Ostuni)
- the food markets — the Mercato Centrale (Florence, Bologna), the Mercato di Porta Portese (Rome), the Mercato di Ballarò (Palermo)
- the cooking classes — the pasta-making in Rome (L'Accademia), the pizza-making in Naples (Sorbillo), the cooking in Bologna (Bolognese cooking school)
- the wine tours — the Chianti (Tuscany), the Barolo (Piedmont), the Brunello (Montalcino), the Prosecco (Veneto)
- the truffle hunting — the Alba (Piedmont) in October–December; the truffle dogs

## Where to stay
- Rome: The St. Regis (Via Veneto, top), The Rome EDITION (Castel Sant'Angelo, top), Hotel de Russie (Via del Babuino, mid), Hotel Artesta (Prati, budget)
- Florence: The St. Regis (Ponte Vecchio, top), Hotel Lungarno (Arno view, top), Hotel Savoy (Duomo, mid), Hotel Roma (Santa Maria Novella, budget)
- Venice: The Gritti Palace (San Marco, top), The Danieli (San Marco, top), Hotel Excelsior (St. Mark's, mid), Hotel Roma (Dorsoduro, budget)
- Amalfi: Le Sirenuse (Positano, top), Hotel Marincanto (Positano, top), Hotel Marina Riviera (Amalfi, mid), Hotel Villa Margherita (Atrani, budget)
- Tuscany: the agriturismi (farm stays) in the Chianti; the Borgo (San Gimignano, mid); the B&B (Siena, budget)
- Sicily: the Grand Hotel Timeo (Taormina, top), the Hotel Splendid (Cefalù, mid), the B&B (Palermo, budget)
- Lake Como: the Grand Hotel Tremezzo (Bellagio, top), the Hotel Sereni (Varenna, mid), the B&B (Como, budget)
- Puglia: the Palazzo (Matera, top), the Borgo (Alberobello, mid), the B&B (Ostuni, budget)

## Good to know
- Many towns empty in August — the islands and north are quieter
- The train makes day trips easy
