## Overview
A small, scenic, mountain country: Bratislava's old town and castle, the High Tatras for skiing and hiking, the fairy-tale castle of Bojnice, and the quiet wine of the Little Carpathians.

## Where to go
Top places: Bratislava, Tatra Mountains, castles, wine region.

## Landmarks & sights
- Bratislava Castle
- the High Tatras
- the Bojnice Castle
- the Little Carpathians (wine)

## Food & drink
- bryndzova halushka (cheese & noodles)
- goulash
- local beer

## Things to do
- the High Tatras (skiing, hiking)
- Bratislava (the old town)
- the castles (Bojnice)
- the wine (the Little Carpathians)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Bratislava City Center Hotel, Poprad High Tatras Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer: the High Tatras; winter: the skiing
- The old town is compact — walk it in a day
