## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** August, October, July — best overall: **August**.
- **Shoulder:** September, June, May.
- **Off-season:** April, March, December, February, January, November.
- Climate reference point: **Sarajevo** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Hot summers and cold winters; areas of high elevation have short, cool summers and long, severe winters; mild, rainy winters along coast.
