## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** May, June, September — best overall: **May**.
- **Shoulder:** August, July, October.
- **Off-season:** April, November, March, February, December, January.
- Climate reference point: **Oslo** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Winter in the Nordic countries 

 
 

Because of the gulf stream, the climate in Norway, especially along the coast, is noticeably warmer than what would otherwise be expected at such a high latitude.
- Along the coast of Hordaland and Rogaland temperatures only occasionally and briefly drop below -5°C (20°F), and the climate is not unlike other coastal areas around the North Sea.
- Some mountain areas have glaciers and permanent snow, but on the mainland there is permafrost (permanently frozen ground) only in some small areas in the high mountains.
- If you come in the beginning of May some passes can be still closed, but since the snow is melting very quickly, you will get a possibility to enjoy plenty of waterfalls before they disappear.
