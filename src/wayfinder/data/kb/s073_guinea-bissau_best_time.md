## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Guinea-Bissau is warm all year around and there is little temperature fluctuation; it averages 26.3°C (79.3°F).
- The average rainfall for Bissau is 2,024 mm although this is almost entirely accounted for during the rainy season which falls between June and September/October.
- From December to April, the country experiences drought.
