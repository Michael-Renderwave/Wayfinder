## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** July, October, May — best overall: **July**.
- **Shoulder:** August, November, June.
- **Off-season:** April, September, December, January, March, February.

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Although Peru is located entirely in the tropical latitudes, the combination of tropical latitude, mountain ranges, topography variations, and two ocean currents (Humboldt and El Niño) gives Peru a large diversity of climates.
- Elevations above sea level in the country range from −37 m to 6,778 m (−121 ft to 22,238 ft) and precipitation ranges from less than 20 mm (0.79 in) annually in desert areas to more than 8,000 mm (310 in) in tropical rainforest areas.
- Peru can be divided into three main climate regions:
*The coastal region has moderate temperatures, low precipitation, and high humidity, except for its warmer, wetter northern reaches.
- The Peruvian Amazon, is characterized by heavy rainfall and high temperatures, except for its southernmost part, which has cold winters and seasonal rainfall.
