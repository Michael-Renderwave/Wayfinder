## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** January, December, November — best overall: **January**.
- **Shoulder:** February, July, April.
- **Off-season:** March, June, August, September, October, May.
- Climate reference point: **Yamoussoukro** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Tropical along coast, semiarid in far north; three seasons - warm and dry (Nov-Mar), hot and dry (Mar-May), hot and wet (Jun-Oct).
- The coast has heavy surf and no natural harbours; during the rainy season torrential flooding is possible.
