## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** February, January, December — best overall: **February**.
- **Shoulder:** March, April, May.
- **Off-season:** June, July, August, September, October, November.
- Climate reference point: **Monrovia** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- The equatorial climate is hot year-round with heavy rainfall from May to October with a short interlude in mid-July to August.
- During the winter months of November to March dry dust-laden harmattan winds blow inland causing many problems for residents.
