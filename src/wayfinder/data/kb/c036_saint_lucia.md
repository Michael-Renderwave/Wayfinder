## Overview
The Pitons island: two volcanic spires rising from the sea, hot springs, rainforest, and some of the Caribbean's most photogenic coastline.

## Where to go
Top places: Castries, Soufrière, Anse Raymond, Rodney Bay, Gros Islet.

## Landmarks & sights
- The Pitons (UNESCO)
- Soufrière hot springs
- Drive to the Top

## Food & drink
- fresh fish & seafood
- callaloo (sautéed leafy greens)
- tropical fruit & local rum

## Things to do
- hot springs (Soufrière)
- hiking the Pitons
- beach days + marine park
- catamaran sailing

## Where to stay

Stay options: Rodney Bay Beach Hotel, Soufrière Hot Spring Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best: December–April
- The Pitons are the icon — plan a day around them
