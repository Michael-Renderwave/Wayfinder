## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** August, June, July — best overall: **August**.
- **Shoulder:** September, October, May.
- **Off-season:** April, March, December, November, February, January.
- Climate reference point: **Chișinău** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Despite its proximity to the Black Sea, Moldova experiences continental cold and snowy winters, mild springs and autumns, and warm to hot summers.
