## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** July, August, June — best overall: **July**.
- **Shoulder:** April, November, May.
- **Off-season:** February, September, December, October, January, March.
- Climate reference point: **Madrid** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Although many visitors travel to Spain for the warm climate, it can be cold in winter, especially in the Central Region and in the North, and in some places it is also rainy in summer.
- Remember to travel with adequate clothes.
- In summer, avoid direct exposure to sunlight for long periods of time to prevent Sunburn and sun protection.
- Drink water, walk on the shady side of street and keep a container of sun cream (suntan lotion) handy.
