## Overview
The sun-and-sea country of the Atlantic: Lisbon's tiled old town, Porto's Douro river and Port wine, the cliffs and coves of the Algarve, and the volcanic islands of Madeira and the Azores. Compact, affordable, and one of the best food-to-price ratios in Europe.

## Where to go
Top places: Lisbon & Sintra, Porto, the Algarve, Madeira, the Azores, Évora, the Douro Valley.

## Landmarks & sights
- Belém Tower (Lisbon, UNESCO) — the 16th-century stone fortress at the Tagus mouth
- the Jerónimos Monastery (Lisbon) — the ornate Manueline cloister; book entry
- the São Jorge Castle (Lisbon) — the hilltop ramparts with a panoramic view
- the Alfama (Lisbon) — the tiled old town; the heart of the "fado"
- the LX Factory (Lisbon) — a riverside creative hub; the famous bookshop
- the Sintra palaces — the Quinta da Regaleira + the Pena Palace; the fairy-tale hilltops
- the Ribeira do Porto — the Douro old town; the "pintxo" streets
- the Livraria Lello (Porto) — the "red" bookshop; a "Porto" icon
- the Douro Valley — the terraced vineyards; a river cruise up the "Port" river
- the Algarve cliffs & sea caves — the Ponta da Piedade cliffs, the Benagil cave (by boat)
- the Madeira levada walks — the cliff-side irrigation trails; the "Pico do Arieiro"
- the Azores volcanic landscapes — the crater lakes of São Miguel, whale & dolphin watching
- the Évora Roman ruins — the "Osseary" chapel and the Roman temple
- the Nazaré & the "big-wave" surf — the famous 2013 "monster wave" at Nazaré
- the Lagos & the "Praia da Marinha" — one of the best beaches in Europe

## Food & drink
- pastel de nata — the custard tart; Pastéis de Belém (Lisbon) or the Manteigaria
- bacalhau à Brás — the shredded cod with egg & potato; the national dish
- grilled sardines (sardinhas) — the summer street staple
- caldo verde — the kale-and-sausage soup; a winter comfort
- bifana — the pork-in-tomato-sauce sandwich; a street-food icon
- francesinha (Porto) — the heavy breaded sandwich; a Porto breakfast
- arroz de marisco — the seafood rice
- pastéis de bacalhau — the cod fritters
- piri-piri chicken (frango) — the chili chicken
- petiscos — the small plates of a Porto bar crawl
- Port wine — the fortified wine of the Douro
- Vinho Verde — the green wine of the Minho
- Alvarinho — the white wine of the Dão/Minho
- guisados — the stews; a "Lisbon" winter staple

## Things to do
- 2-day Lisbon: Belém + the Alfama + the LX Factory + a fado show
- a Sintra day trip: the Quinta da Regaleira + the Pena Palace
- 2-day Porto: the Ribeira + the Livraria Lello + a Port tasting
- a Douro Valley river cruise: the vineyards + a Port tasting
- the Algarve: a beach week in Lagos or Albufeira
- a Madeira levada walk: the Pico do Arieiro + the Caldeirinha
- the Azores: whale & dolphin watching + the crater lakes of São Miguel
- a Nazaré surf day: the "monster wave" + the lighthouse
- the Praia da Marinha (Lagos) — one of the best beaches in Europe
- a Douro Valley hike: the terraced vineyards
- a surf day in the Algarve or Nazaré
- a wine day in the Douro Valley

## Good to know
- Summer is hot in the Algarve (35°C+); the north (Porto, Braga) is cooler
- The "pastel de nata" is the highlight — go early, before the "lunch" rush
- The "fado" (the "sad" music) is the "Portuguese" soul — go to a "casa de fado" in Lisbon or Porto
- Book the "Sintra" palaces ahead — they sell out
- Tipping ~5–10% is polite but not expected — it's in the bill
- The "siesta" (15:00–17:00) is real in smaller towns; shops close
- The "Port" wine is the "Douro" heart — go to the "Douro Valley" for a tasting
