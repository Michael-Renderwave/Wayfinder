## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** November, October, December — best overall: **November**.
- **Shoulder:** February, April, January.
- **Off-season:** March, May, September, August, July, June.
- Climate reference point: **New Delhi** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- The Southwest monsoon is the more important one, as it causes rains over most parts of the country, and is the crucial variable that decides how the crops will do.
- The Southwest monsoon hits the west coast the most, as crossing the Western Ghats and reaching the rest of India is an uphill task for the winds.
- The Northeast monsoon hits the east coast between October and February, mostly in the form of occasional cyclones that cause much devastation every year.
- India experiences at least three seasons a year, Summer, Rainy Season (or "Monsoon") and Winter, though in the tropical South calling the 25°C (77°F) weather "Winter" would be stretching the concept.
