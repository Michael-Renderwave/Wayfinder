## Overview
South America's giant: Rio's beaches and mountains, Amazon rainforest, Iguazú Falls, Carnival, and some of the world's best churrascos — enormous in scale and energy.

## Where to go
Top places: Brasília, Rio de Janeiro, São Paulo, Salvador, Manaus, Florianópolis, Foz do Iguaçu, Ouro Preto.

## Landmarks & sights
- Christ the Redeemer (Cristo Redentor) — 30 m statue atop Corcovado, best at sunrise or sunset
- Sugarloaf Mountain (Pão de Açúcar) — cable car from Porto de Catacões, 2 stops to the top
- Iguazú Falls — 275 falls on the Brazilian side (larger than the Argentine side); walkway system
- Copacabana Beach — 4.5 km of sand, famous mosaic wave pattern on the sidewalk
- Ipanema Beach — where Bob Marley and Janis Joplin strolled; surf-friendly
- Leblon Beach — quieter, upscale; great for morning swims
- Tijuca National Park — Atlantic forest inside the city, hiking trails to the peaks
- Lapa district — cascading rock formations, famous nightlife & samba clubs (Rio Samba Club, Boteco do Alemão)
- Santa Teresa neighborhood — hillside village with the historic Tram, artisan shops, sunset views
- Pelourinho (Salvador) — colonial-era square, Afro-Brazilian culture, capoeira shows daily
- Chapada Diamantina — waterfalls, crystal-clear rivers, hiking (Paracatu, Pratinha)
- Lençóis Maranhenses — white sand dunes with crystal-clear rainwater lagoons

## Food & drink
- feijoada — black bean & pork stew, Saturday tradition in Rio; go to Boteco do Alemão
- churrasco rodízio — unlimited grilled meats on a stick; Fogo de Chão, Figueira
- açaí bowls — thick purple açaí with granola, banana; found everywhere in the north
- caipirinha — cachaça + lime + sugar; the national cocktail
- brigadeiro — chocolate truffle with sprinkles; buy at Doceria São José (Rio)
- moqueca — coconut-milk fish stew (Bahian style with dendê oil, capixaba style without)
- pastéis — golden fried pastry; street food in Rio, Salvador, São Paulo
- pao de queijo — cheese bread; breakfast staple at Padaria do Mané
- acarajé — black-eyed pea fritter with vatapá; street food in Salvador
- brigadeiro + cocada — traditional sweets at any doceria
- pão de mel — honey cake; try at Doceria da Lapa (São Paulo)
- cachaça tours — in the cachaça valley (Vale do Aço, Minas Gerais)

## Things to do
- Rio: 2-day combo of Cristo + Sugarloaf + Tijuca park + Lapa at night
- Amazon: 2–4 day jungle lodge from Manaus; piranha fishing, indigenous village visits
- samba schools: visit the Portela or Mangueira school (Rio) for daily rehearsals
- Carnival (Rio, Feb/Mar) — Sambódromo parade, books 6+ months ahead
- surf at Florianópolis (Joaquina, Campeche beaches)
- wine country (Serra Gaúcha) — boutique wineries, 2 hr from Porto Alegre
- Buzios — boutique beach town, 90 min from Rio; 24 beaches
- Jericoacoara — kitesurf capital, desert-like dunes meeting the sea (Ceará)
- Parnaiba dunes + Lagoa do Paraíso (Maranhão)
- Pantanal safari (Cuiabá) — jaguar, capybara, caiman spotting
- Lençóis Maranhenses — hike the dunes, swim the lagoons (Jun–Sep)

## Where to stay

Stay options:
- Rio: Figueira (Ipanema), Belmond Copacabana Palace, Rio City Center Hotel (budget)
- Salvador: Fairmont Rio Mar (Pituba), RioMar Hotel (center)
- São Paulo: W São Paulo (Itaim), Hilton (Vila Olímpia), Ibis (Pinheiros, budget)
- Florianópolis: Pestana Palace (Jurerê), Villa Galé (Jazidas)
- Foz do Iguaçu: Renaissance Iguassu Falls, Radisson (budget, near falls)
- Chapada Diamantina: pousadas in Lençóis or Palmeira (eco-lodges)

## Good to know
- Carnival (Rio, Feb/Mar) books out months ahead
- Summer is December–February
