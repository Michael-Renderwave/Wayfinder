## Overview
A long, narrow country of deep fjords, the dramatic Lofoten islands, the northern lights of Tromso, and the quiet, forested interior — a scenic, expensive, unforgettable base.

## Where to go
Top places: Oslo, fjords, Lofoten, Tromso, Stavanger.

## Landmarks & sights
- the Geirangerfjord (UNESCO)
- the Lofoten
- Bryggen (Bergen, UNESCO)
- the prehistoric stave churches

## Food & drink
- king crab (the Lofoten)
- smorrebrod
- fresh seafood
- cardamom buns (kneipp)

## Things to do
- the fjords (Geiranger)
- northern lights (Tromso, Sept–March)
- the Lofoten
- the midnight sun (summer)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Oslo City Center Hotel, Bergen Harbor Hotel, Svolvar Lofoten Hotel.

## Good to know
- Northern lights: September–March
- The fjords are best in the summer
