## Overview
A Mediterranean island of Greek Orthodox painted churches, Pafos' ancient rock and tombs, and long sandy beaches around a warm, wine-rich coast.

## Where to go
Top places: Nicosia, Paphos, Limassol, Larnaca, Troodos, Ayia Napa.

## Landmarks & sights
- Pafos Rock
- Bellapais Abbey
- Troodos painted churches (UNESCO)

## Food & drink
- halloumi
- souvlaki & meze
- fresh seafood
- Cypriot wine (Xynistra)

## Things to do
- beach hopping (Ayia Napa)
- diving the reefs
- Troodos mountain hiking
- the ghost town of Pyla

## Where to stay

Stay options: Limassol Beachfront Hotel, Paphos Old Town Hotel.

## Good to know
- May–June and September–October are the sweet spots
- The north side requires a separate entry — plan routes accordingly
