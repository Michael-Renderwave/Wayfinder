## Overview
The world's oldest republic: the three towers of the City of San Marino, the mountain of the Titano, and the quiet, compact old town — a small, historic, Italian-adjacent base.

## Where to go
Top places: City of San Marino, Titano, Borgo Maggiore.

## Landmarks & sights
- the three towers (UNESCO)
- the Titano (mountain)
- the old town

## Food & drink
- local Italian cuisine
- fresh pasta
- the local wine (Vermentino)

## Things to do
- the three towers
- the Titano (mountain)
- the old town (the center)

## Where to stay

Stay options: San Marino Old Town Hotel.

## Good to know
- A day trip from Italy (Rimini / Urbino) works well
- The old town is compact — walk it in a day
