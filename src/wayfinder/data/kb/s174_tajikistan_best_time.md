## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** June, September, August — best overall: **June**.
- **Shoulder:** July, October, November.
- **Off-season:** May, April, December, March, January, February.
- Climate reference point: **Dushanbe** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- This can be compared with the climate of northern Greece, central Italy, northern Spain or partly southern France, central Turkey, or with the climate of northern California or Colorado.
- Nevertheless, such temperatures are easier to tolerate here, since the air here is very dry, not humid, as, for example, in India, Thailand, Malaysia and similar countries.
- It is most comfortable to visit this country from mid-May to early June or from August to early September, when it is not cool, as in winter, but also not very hot, as in the height of summer.
- Being at the mouth of a long valley with a wide open desert to the west, the area around Khujand is usually very windy, which makes the temperature feel even cooler.
