## Overview
South America's dramatic range: Patagonian glaciers, Iguazú Falls, world-class wine, and Buenos Aires — tango, steakhouses, and grand European architecture.

## Where to go
Top places: Buenos Aires, Patagonia, Iguazú Falls, Mendoza, Bariloche, Salta.

## Landmarks & sights
- Perito Moreno Glacier
- Iguazú Falls
- Aconcagua (world's highest outside Asia)
- Obelisco & Recoleta (Buenos Aires)

## Food & drink
- asado (slow-grilled beef)
- empanadas
- Malbec wine (Mendoza)

## Things to do
- glacier trekking (El Calafate)
- wine country (Mendoza)
- tango in Buenos Aires
- Patagonian hiking & fjords

## Where to stay

Stay options: Buenos Aires City Center Hotel, Recoleta Boutique Hotel, El Calafate Airport Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best: December–March (southern-hemisphere summer)
- Carry small bills — card coverage is patchy outside big cities
