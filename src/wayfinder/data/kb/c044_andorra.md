## Overview
A tiny Pyrenean principality and a winter-sports base: Grandvalira's big ski domain, Andorra la Vella — Europe's highest capital — and tax-free shopping.

## Where to go
Top places: Andorra la Vella, Soldeu, Pal, Escaldes-Engordany.

## Landmarks & sights
- Grandvalira ski area
- Sant Joan de Caselles church

## Food & drink
- trinxat (hearty mountain stew)
- roasted lamb
- duty-free chocolate

## Things to do
- skiing & snowboarding (Grandvalira)
- summer hiking & mountain biking
- duty-free shopping

## Where to stay
- Soldeu (ski village base)
Stay options: Soldeu Mountain Hotel, Andorra la Vella City Hotel.

## Good to know
- Ski season: December–April
- No airport — you arrive by road from France or Spain
