## Overview
South America's last frontier: a Dutch-colonial city (Paramaribo), a UNESCO rainforest, and Maroon cultures — wild, quiet, and unspoiled.

## Where to go
Top places: Paramaribo, Central Suriname Nature Reserve, Kootstreek, Marowijne River.

## Landmarks & sights
- Central Suriname Rainforest (UNESCO)
- Paramaribo colonial architecture (UNESCO)
- Marowijne River

## Food & drink
- roti (flatbread with curry)
- pom (cassava & fish)
- fresh river fish
- tropical fruit & local rum

## Things to do
- rainforest treks (Central Suriname)
- river cruises (Marowijne)
- Maroon culture & drumming
- birdwatching (300+ species)

## Where to stay
- jungle lodges (Central Suriname)
Stay options: Paramaribo City Hotel, Central Suriname Jungle Lodge.

## Good to know
- Year-round rainforest — dry season Nov–Mar is best
- A true off-the-beaten-path destination — few visitors
