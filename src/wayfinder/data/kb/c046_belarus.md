## Overview
A quiet, forested corner of Eastern Europe: Minsk's wide boulevards, Brest's storied fortress, and UNESCO castle towns — with primeval forests at the Białowieża border.

## Where to go
Top places: Minsk, Brest, Mir, Nesvizh.

## Landmarks & sights
- Mir & Nesvizh castles (UNESCO)
- Cathedral Square (Minsk)
- Brest Fortress (UNESCO)

## Food & drink
- draniki (potato pancakes)
- machanka (rich stew)
- kolduny (dumplings)
- honey vodka

## Things to do
- castles & palaces (Mir, Nesvizh)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Minsk City Center Hotel, Brest Old Town Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best: late May–September
- Carry cash — card coverage is patchy in smaller towns
