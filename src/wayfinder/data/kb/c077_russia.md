## Overview
The world's biggest country: Moscow's Red Square and the Kremlin, St. Petersburg's Hermitage and canals, the deep blue of the Lake Baikal, and the quiet, forested interior of the Karelia.

## Where to go
Top places: Moscow, St. Petersburg, Golden Ring, Baikal, Karelia.

## Landmarks & sights
- the Hermitage (St. Petersburg)
- the Lake Baikal
- the Kizhi Island (UNESCO)

## Food & drink
- borscht
- pelmeni (dumplings)
- blini (crepes)
- vodka & local beer

## Things to do
- the Hermitage (St. Petersburg)
- the Red Square (Moscow)
- the Lake Baikal
- the Karelia (nature)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Moscow City Center Hotel, St. Petersburg City Center Hotel, Listvyanka Baikal Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer: the Baikal; the high season is June–August
- The cities are the highlight — the museums are world-class
