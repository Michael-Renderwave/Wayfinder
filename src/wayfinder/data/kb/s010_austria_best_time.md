## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** August, October, July — best overall: **August**.
- **Shoulder:** June, May, March.
- **Off-season:** April, February, November, September, December, January.
- Climate reference point: **Vienna** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Summers last from early June to mid-September and
can be hot in some years and rainy in others.
- Day-time temperatures in July and August are around 25°C (77°F), but can often reach 35°C (95°F).
- Winters are cold in the lowlands and very harsh in the Alpine region with temperatures often dropping below -10°C (14°F).
- In the Alpine region large temperature fluctuations occur all year round and nights are chilly even in high summer.
