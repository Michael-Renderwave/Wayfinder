## Overview
A long, thin country of extremes: the driest desert on Earth (Atacama), Patagonian granite towers, wine valleys, and the remote Moai of Easter Island.

## Where to go
Top places: Santiago, Atacama, Valparaíso, Torres del Paine, Easter Island, Puerto Natales.

## Landmarks & sights
- Moai statues (Easter Island)
- Torres del Paine granite towers
- Atacama salt flats & geysers
- Valle de la Luna

## Food & drink
- empanadas de pino
- pastitutos
- coquilles (shellfish soup)
- pisco sour
- Maipo Valley wine

## Things to do
- stargazing in the Atacama
- volcano hikes (Ojos del Salado)
- surfing (Arica, Valparaíso)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Santiago City Center Hotel, San Pedro de Atacama Lodge, Puerto Natales Airport Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer (Dec–Feb) for Patagonia; May–Sep for the Atacama
- Easter Island flights sell out months ahead
