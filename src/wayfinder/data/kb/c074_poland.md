## Overview
A big, varied country of the rebuilt old town of Warsaw, the medieval center of Krakow, the Tatra Mountains, and the Hanza old town of Gdansk — a rich, affordable, food-rich base.

## Where to go
Top places: Warsaw, Krakow, Tatra Mountains, Wroclaw, Gdansk, Baltyk coast.

## Landmarks & sights
- the Old Town (Warsaw, UNESCO)
- Wawel Castle (Krakow, UNESCO)
- the Tatra Mountains
- the Hanza old town (Gdansk)

## Food & drink
- pierogi
- bigos (hunter's stew)
- goziki
- vodka & local beer

## Things to do
- the castles (Krakow)
- the Tatra Mountains (Zakopane)
- the old town (Warsaw)
- the Baltyk coast (Gdansk)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Warsaw City Center Hotel, Krakow Old Town Hotel, Zakopane Tatra Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer: June–August
- The old towns are the highlight — walk them
