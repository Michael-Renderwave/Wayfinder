## Overview
Baltic's Art Nouveau capital: Riga's ornate center, the quiet seaside of Jurmala, the medieval castle of Sigulda, and the long, dune-covered Curonian Lagoon.

## Where to go
Top places: Riga, Sigulda, Curonian Lagoon, Jurmala.

## Landmarks & sights
- Riga's Art Nouveau center (UNESCO)
- the House of the Blackheads
- the Sigulda castle

## Food & drink
- herring & potato
- black bread (melnie maizes)
- meadow (medus) cake
- mead

## Things to do
- Baltic beaches (Jurmala)
- cycling (Sigulda)
- sauna

## Where to stay

Stay options: Riga Old Town Hotel, Jurmala Seaside Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer: June–August
- The Art Nouveau walking tour is a Riga classic
