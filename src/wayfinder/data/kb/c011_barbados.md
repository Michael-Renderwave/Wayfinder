## Overview
A British-style Caribbean island with a 132-year-old capital (Bridgetown), famous for its kitesurfing, flying fish, and laid-back Bajan culture.

## Where to go
Top places: Bridgetown, Holetown, Sandy Bay, Christ Church.

## Landmarks & sights
- Holetown Historic District (UNESCO)
- Bathsheba rock formations
- Harrison's Cave

## Food & drink
- cou-cou & flying fish
- pepper pot
- fresh seafood & local rum

## Things to do
- kitesurfing at Sandy Bay
- turtle hatchery visits
- rum distillery tours
- west-coast beach days

## Where to stay

Stay options: Bridgetown City Hotel, Sandy Bay Beach Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best: December–April (calm trade winds)
- East coast is rough January–October; the west coast is the calm side
