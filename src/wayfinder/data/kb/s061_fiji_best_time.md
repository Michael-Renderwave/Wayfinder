## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** July, October, June — best overall: **July**.
- **Shoulder:** August, September, December.
- **Off-season:** April, May, November, January, February, March.
- Climate reference point: **Suva** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Tropical marine; only slight seasonal temperature variation.
- Tropical cyclonic storms (the South Pacific version of hurricanes) can occur from November-April.
- Temperature sensitive visitors may wish to visit during the Southern Hemisphere winter.
