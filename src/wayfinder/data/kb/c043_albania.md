## Overview
A rugged, affordable Adriatic gem: Butrint's UNESCO ruins, the Blue Eye spring, the stone old towns of Berat and Gjirokastër, and a young Riviera of islands and coves.

## Where to go
Top places: Tirana, Durrës, Berat, Gjirokastër, Ksamil, Butrint.

## Landmarks & sights
- Butrint (UNESCO)
- Blue Eye spring
- Rozafa Castle
- Gjirokastër old town (UNESCO)

## Food & drink
- byrek (baked pastry)
- fresh seafood
- raki

## Things to do
- Albanian Riviera beaches
- rafting the Drin river

## Where to stay

Stay options: Tirana City Center Hotel, Durrës Beachfront Hotel, Gjirokastër Old Town Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best: May–June or September–October
- Carry cash — cards are patchy in the south
