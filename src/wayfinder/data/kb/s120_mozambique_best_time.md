## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** July, June, August — best overall: **July**.
- **Shoulder:** May, October, September.
- **Off-season:** April, January, March, November, December, February.
- Climate reference point: **Maputo** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Maputo
 

Almost all of Mozambique is within the tropics, so Mozambique features a mostly tropical climate.
- Evenings are rarely cold, except for a few nights in June and July and the rainfall isnt too high.
- The interior plains generally have a higher temperature than that of the coast and have higher rainfall throughout the year.
- For up-to date weather forecasts and tide tables visit http://www.climateandweather.com/weather-in-mozambique date=May 2024.
