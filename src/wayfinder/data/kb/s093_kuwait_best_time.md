## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** April, November, October — best overall: **April**.
- **Shoulder:** December, February, January.
- **Off-season:** March, May, September, June, August, July.
- Climate reference point: **Kuwait City** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Dry desert; intensely hot summers; short, cool winters.
- Natural hazards : sudden cloudbursts are common from October to April; they occasionally bring heavy rain which can, in some rare cases, damage roads and houses; sandstorms and dust storms occur throughout the year, but are most common between March and August.
- Common temperatures range from 5°C in Dec/Jan to over 50°C from Jun to Aug.
- It would be prudent for travellers to pack clothes accordingly.
