## Overview
Vienna's palaces and music, Salzburg's Mozart legacy, and the Alps: Hallstatt's lakeside village, Innsbruck's peaks, and the most famous ski valleys in Europe.

## Where to go
Top places: Vienna, Salzburg, Innsbruck, Hallstatt, Graz, Lake Wolfgang.

## Landmarks & sights
- Schönbrunn Palace
- St. Stephen's Cathedral (Vienna)
- Hallstatt (UNESCO-adjacent lakeside)
- Hohensalzburg Fortress

## Food & drink
- Sachertorte (chocolate cake)
- Wiener Schnitzel
- Kaiserschmarrn (caramelized pancake)
- Alpine huts & Jausen

## Things to do
- classical concerts (Vienna)
- Alpine lakes & Hallstatt
- skiing (Innsbruck / St. Anton)
- cycling the Danube

## Where to stay
- Vienna (Innere Stadt / Mariahilfer)
Stay options: Vienna City Center Hotel, Salzburg Old Town Hotel, Hallstatt Alpine Hotel.

## Good to know
- Vienna museums are cheap or free on the first Sunday of many months
- Summer: June–September; winter: December–March for skiing
