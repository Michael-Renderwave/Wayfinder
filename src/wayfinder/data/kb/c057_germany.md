## Overview
From Berlin's wall and museums to Bavaria's castles and beer, the Rhine's castle-dotted river, and the Black Forest's quiet lakes — a big, varied, easy-to-train country.

## Where to go
Top places: Berlin, Munich, Rhine valley, Bavarian Alps, Hamburg, Black Forest, Saxon Switzerland.

## Landmarks & sights
- Brandenburg Gate (Berlin)
- Neuschwanstein Castle
- Rhine castles (Lorelei)
- Nuremberg old town

## Food & drink
- bratwurst & pretzels
- schnitzel
- currywurst (Berlin)
- beer halls (Munich)

## Things to do
- Oktoberfest (late August–September)
- Rhine valley cycling
- castle hopping
- Baltic beaches (Usedom)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Berlin City Center Hotel, Munich Old Town Hotel, Cologne Riverside Hotel.

## Good to know
- Regional trains (RE) cover the Rhine valley well
- Oktoberfest is late August–early September
