## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** February, January, March — best overall: **February**.
- **Shoulder:** April, June, July.
- **Off-season:** December, May, August, September, October, November.
- Climate reference point: **Majuro** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Wet season from May to November; hot and humid; islands border typhoon belt.
