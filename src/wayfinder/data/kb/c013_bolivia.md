## Overview
Andean Bolivia: the otherworldly Salar de Uyuni, colonial Sucre, high-altitude La Paz, and Amazonian lowlands — one of South America's most varied landscapes.

## Where to go
Top places: La Paz, Salar de Uyuni, Sucre, Potosí, Lake Titicaca.

## Landmarks & sights
- Casa de la Moneda (Potosí)

## Food & drink
- salteñas (savory empanadas)
- silpancho
- api con chicha (corn beer)
- chairo stew

## Things to do
- Andean markets (Alasitas, Witches' Market)
- Amazon tours from Río Pilo
- Titicaca boat trips

## Where to stay

Stay options: La Paz City Center Hotel, Uyuni Salt Flat Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best: May–September (dry)
- Altitude is real — take the first 1–2 days slow (coca tea helps)
