## Overview
A 30-island chain in the southern Caribbean: Bequia's barefoot culture, Union Island's calm bays, and the chocolate-colored Chocolate Hole — sailing paradise.

## Where to go
Top places: Kingstown, St. Vincent, Bequia, Union Island, Canouan.

## Landmarks & sights
- Chocolate Hole (The Grenadines)
- Mayon River (St. Vincent)
- Union Island's calm bays

## Food & drink
- fresh fish & seafood
- funchie (fried cornmeal)
- tropical fruit & local rum

## Things to do
- island hopping (The Grenadines)
- sailing & catamarans
- diving
- quiet beach days

## Where to stay

Stay options: Kingstown City Hotel, Bequia Island Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best: December–April
- Sailing capital of the Caribbean — book a catamaran week
