## Overview
The 'Nature Island' of the Caribbean: hot springs, a boiling lake, dense rainforest, and some of the best diving in the Eastern Caribbean — deliberately unspoiled.

## Where to go
Top places: Roseau, Soufrière, Morne Bruce, Windsor waterfall.

## Landmarks & sights
- Morne Trois Pitons National Park
- Windsor waterfall

## Food & drink
- fresh fish & dumplings
- callaloo
- tropical fruit & local rum

## Things to do
- freshwater swimming (Windsor)
- hot springs (Soufrière)
- hiking the 'Nature Island'
- wreck diving (CDM)

## Where to stay
- south-coast guesthouses
Stay options: Roseau City Hotel, Soufrière Hot Spring Lodge.

## Good to know
- Best: October–April (trade-wind season)
- Limited flights — book several months ahead
