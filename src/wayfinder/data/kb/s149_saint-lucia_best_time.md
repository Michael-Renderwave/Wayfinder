## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** April, March, May — best overall: **April**.
- **Shoulder:** February, December, October.
- **Off-season:** January, September, November, August, June, July.
- Climate reference point: **Castries** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Tropical, moderated by northeast trade winds; dry season from January through April, rainy season from May through September; The most recent hurricane tocause damage in St.
- Lucia was Beryl (end of June, 2024), although this storm was downgraded to a tropical storm, as it reached St Lucias shores.
- Prior to this storm, Hurricane Thomas caused considerable damage in 2014.
