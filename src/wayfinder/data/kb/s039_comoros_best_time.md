## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** June, October, May — best overall: **June**.
- **Shoulder:** July, September, March.
- **Off-season:** January, February, April, August, November, December.
- Climate reference point: **Moroni** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Located just south of the equator, the Comoros Islands have a tropical maritime climate, characterized by two seasons according to rainfall.
- The best season to visit the Comoros is between May and November, during the dry season when the climate is cool and relatively dry, while from December to April is hot and humid.
- The maximum temperature may vary between 31ºC in December and 27ºC in August, while the average minimum vary between 23ºC in February and 19ºC in July.
