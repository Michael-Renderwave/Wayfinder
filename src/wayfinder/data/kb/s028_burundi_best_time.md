## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** July, August, June — best overall: **July**.
- **Shoulder:** September, May, February.
- **Off-season:** October, March, January, November, April, December.
- Climate reference point: **Gitega** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Temperature also varies considerably from one region to another, chiefly as a result of differences in altitude.
- The area around Lake Tanganyika is warmer, averaging 23 ; the highest mountain areas are cooler, averaging 16.
- Dry seasons vary in length, and there are sometimes long periods of drought.
- However, four seasons can be distinguished: the long dry season (June–August), the short wet season (September–November), the short dry season (December–January), and the long wet season (February–May).
