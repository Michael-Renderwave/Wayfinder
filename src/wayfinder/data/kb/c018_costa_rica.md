## Overview
Pura Vida: a compact nature capital of volcanoes, cloud forests, sloths, and two coastlines — the Caribbean's best-wildlife base.

## Where to go
Top places: San José, Manuel Antonio, Arenal, Monteverde, Tortuguero, Tamarindo, Osa Peninsula.

## Landmarks & sights
- Manuel Antonio National Park
- Arenal Volcano
- Monteverde Cloud Forest

## Food & drink
- gallo pinto
- casado (rice, beans, egg, plantain)
- fresh tropical fruit
- grilled seafood

## Things to do
- zip-lining & sloth spotting
- surfing (Tamarindo, Santa Teresa)
- hot springs (Arenal)
- whale watching (Dec–Aug)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Manuel Antonio Beach Hotel, Arenal Volcano Hotel, Tamarindo Surf Hotel.

## Good to know
- Dry season: December–April; green season (May–Nov) is lush and cheaper
- 'Pura Vida' is a real travel philosophy here
