## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** March, April, February — best overall: **March**.
- **Shoulder:** December, September, January.
- **Off-season:** May, October, November, August, June, July.
- Climate reference point: **Kingstown** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Tropical; little seasonal temperature variation; rainy season (May to November).
- Hurricanes threaten the islands from July to October.
