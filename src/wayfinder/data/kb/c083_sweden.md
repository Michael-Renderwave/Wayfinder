## Overview
A long, narrow, forested country: Stockholm's archipelago, the Gothenburg harbor, the Lapland for the northern lights, and the quiet, sandy beaches of the Gotland.

## Where to go
Top places: Stockholm, Gothenburg, Lapland, island of Gotland, Uppsala.

## Landmarks & sights
- Stockholm's archipelago
- Gothenburg's harbor
- the Lapland (northern lights)
- the island of Gotland

## Food & drink
- Swedish meatballs
- herring
- cardamom buns
- local beer & cider

## Things to do
- Stockholm's archipelago
- the Lapland (northern lights, Sept–March)
- Gotland's beaches
- Uppsala's old town

## Where to stay

Stay options: Stockholm City Center Hotel, Gothenburg Harbor Hotel, Abisko Lapland Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer: the Gotland; the high season is June–August
- The northern lights: September–March
