Dining in George Town centers on fresh catch. Try a Cayman-style fish fry at one of the beachfront spots on the East End Road: whole grilled snapper or grouper with fried plantains, conch fritters, and a cold Carib beer, for about $15–$35 per person. Upscale options include The Westin Grand Cayman's Ocean Grill and the harbor-front restaurants on Bath Street, where a three-course dinner runs $60–$120.

Coffee and breakfast are well covered by the island bakery scene on Queen Street; a sit-down breakfast costs $8–$18. For late-night food, the 24-hour spots near the airport and the George Town taxi stand serve conch and rice.

Safety: the Cayman Islands are consistently among the safest Caribbean destinations, with very low violent crime. The main caution is petty theft — keep valuables out of sight on the beach and in George Town's tourist strips, and use hotel safes for passports and cash.

Getting around: taxis are the default and are cheap by US standards ($5–$30 for most island hops). Buses run on a fixed schedule with limited stops, so taxis are recommended for beach days. E-bikes and scooters can be rented in George Town for about $35–$50 per day.
