## Overview
A small, unspoiled island pair: St. Kitts' Brimstone Hill fortress and rainforest, and Nevis — the 'Calm Island' — for slow beach days.

## Where to go
Top places: Basseterre, St. Kitts, Nevis, Red Hook.

## Landmarks & sights
- Brimstone Hill Fortress (UNESCO)
- Nevis' calm bays
- Red Hook harbor

## Food & drink
- fresh seafood
- macaroni pie
- tropical fruit & local rum

## Things to do
- calm-bay days (Nevis)
- diving & snorkeling
- volcano hikes (St. Kitts)
- quiet luxury

## Where to stay

Stay options: Frégate Beach Hotel, Nevis Bay Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best: December–April
- Very quiet — ideal for a slow island week
