## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** July, June, August — best overall: **July**.
- **Shoulder:** April, December, November.
- **Off-season:** January, October, February, September, May, March.
- Climate reference point: **Rome** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- The climate of Italy varies and often differs from the stereotypical Mediterranean climates it is known for.
- For instance, on a typical winter day, you may very well have 6°C in Milan (north), 15°C in Rome (centre), and 21°C in Palermo (south).
- Conditions on the peninsulas coastal areas can be very different from the higher ground and valleys of the interior; during the winter months, the higher altitudes tend to be cold, and during the summer months the heat is oppressive in the valleys.
