## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** August, December, January — best overall: **August**.
- **Shoulder:** February, June, March.
- **Off-season:** May, April, July, September, November, October.
- Climate reference point: **Tarawa** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Whoever coined the phrase, "Its not the heat, its the humidity" may have had Kiribati in mind.
- The average high temperatures are quite reasonable compared to other well-known places in the tropics (such as Bangkok, Singapore, Manila).
- But the humidity more than makes up for this, making it feel very sauna-like.
- The wet season varies, but is usually December to March, give or take a month.
