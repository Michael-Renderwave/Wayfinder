## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** March, February, January — best overall: **March**.
- **Shoulder:** April, November, December.
- **Off-season:** October, July, September, August, June, May.
- Climate reference point: **Manila** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Mountainous areas are the exception to the norm, rather temperate with mildly cool temperatures during the cool dry season from November to March.
- Temperatures are cooler in the mountains, but even lowland areas can experience temperatures below 20 when the northeast monsoon (amihan) from Siberia is at full blast, so bring a sweater or light jacket at these times, especially when walking at night.
- This season is the best time to visit, with drier weather, but flights, boat and ferry trips, buses and accommodations tend to be expensive and difficult, especially during the Christmas and New Year season.
- This also when the southwest monsoon (habagat) blows, which brings rain that characterizes this season.
