## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** August, June, May — best overall: **August**.
- **Shoulder:** September, April, October.
- **Off-season:** July, February, March, December, November, January.
- Climate reference point: **Brussels** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Temperate; mild winters with cool summers.
- Generally rather rainy, humid and cloudy.
- Belgiums average annual temperature in the decade between 1976 and 2006 was 10 °C — a somewhat meaningless measure for non-meteorologists.

- Labour Day — 1 May (fixed)

- Belgian National Day — 21 July (fixed)
