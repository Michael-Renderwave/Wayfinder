## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** April, June, October — best overall: **April**.
- **Shoulder:** March, September, May.
- **Off-season:** November, August, July, February, January, December.
- Climate reference point: **Beijing** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Hainan Island is roughly at the same latitude as Jamaica, while Harbin, one of the largest cities in the north, is at roughly the latitude of Montreal and has the climate to match.
- Once you leave eastern China and enter the majestic Tibetan highlands or the vast steppes and deserts of Gansu, Inner Mongolia and Xinjiang, distances are vast and the land is harsh.
- The rule was relaxed long ago, but the effects are still visible; buildings in the north are provided with steam-based central heating by the government, while buildings in the south are not and instead have to rely on private air-conditioning units for heating.
- The Pearl River Delta region around Guangzhou and Hong Kong and the Along the Yangtze river around Shanghai are major global economic powerhouses, as is the North China plain around Beijing and the Yellow River.
