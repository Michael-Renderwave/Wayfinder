## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** December, January, February — best overall: **December**.
- **Shoulder:** November, March, April.
- **Off-season:** October, September, May, August, June, July.
- Climate reference point: **Hanoi** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- During the rainy season, downpours can happen every afternoon, and occasional street flooding occurs.
- Mosquitoes are most numerous in the rainy season.
- In the Central Coast (Vietnam) regions the Hai Van pass separates two different weather patterns of the north starting in Langco (which is hotter in summer and cooler in winter) from the milder conditions south starting in Da Nang.
- Northeast monsoon conditions Sep-Feb often have strong winds, large sea swells and rain.
