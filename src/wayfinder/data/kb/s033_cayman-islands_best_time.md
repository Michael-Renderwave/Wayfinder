## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** January, February, December — best overall: **January**.
- **Shoulder:** April, March, May.
- **Off-season:** July, August, October, November, June, September.
- Climate reference point: **George Town** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- The climate is tropical marine, featuring warm, rainy summers (May to October) and cool, relatively dry winters (November to April).
- In 2004 the Cayman Islands, especially Grand Cayman, were hit hard by Hurricane Ivan.
