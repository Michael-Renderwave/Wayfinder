## Overview
Baltic's Baroque capital: Vilnius' old town, the sand dunes of the Curonian Spit, the island castle of Trakai, and the quiet, lake-filled interior.

## Where to go
Top places: Vilnius, Curonian Spit, Trakai, Kaunas.

## Landmarks & sights
- Vilnius old town (UNESCO)
- Trakai Island Castle
- the Curonian Spit dunes (UNESCO)

## Food & drink
- cepeleinai (dumplings with meat)
- herring
- black bread
- honey cakes

## Things to do
- Baltic beaches (Nida)
- cycling (the Curonian Spit)
- sauna
- the lake (Trakai)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Vilnius Old Town Hotel, Nida Seaside Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer: June–August
- The spit is a UNESCO site — the dunes are a highlight
