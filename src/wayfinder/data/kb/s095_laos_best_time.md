## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** December, February, January — best overall: **December**.
- **Shoulder:** March, November, April.
- **Off-season:** October, July, August, September, June, May.
- Climate reference point: **Vientiane** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- The hot season is from Mar-May, when temperatures can soar as high as 40°C and the humidity makes it feel like 50°C.
- The slightly cooler wet season is from May-Oct, when temperatures are around 30°C, tropical downpours are frequent (especially Jul-Aug), and some years the Mekong floods.
- The dry season from Nov-Mar, which has low rainfall and temperatures as low as 15°C (or even to zero in the mountains at night), is "high season".
- However, towards the end of the dry season, the northern parts of Laos — basically everything north of Luang Prabang — can become very hazy due to farmers burning fields and fires in the forests.
