## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** January, February, December — best overall: **January**.
- **Shoulder:** August, March, April.
- **Off-season:** November, May, June, July, September, October.
- Climate reference point: **Yaoundé** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Varies with terrain, from tropical along the coast to semiarid and hot in the north.
- If you are going during the summer, plan on lots of rain every day.
- It might be cold up in the mountains, especially at night.
