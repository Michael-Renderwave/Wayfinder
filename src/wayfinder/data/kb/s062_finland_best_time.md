## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** May, June, July — best overall: **May**.
- **Shoulder:** August, September, October.
- **Off-season:** April, November, March, December, February, January.
- Climate reference point: **Helsinki** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Winter in the Nordic Countries 

Located at 60–70°N, Finland has a temperate to subarctic climate, mostly cool or cold but not as cold as you might think, thanks to the moderating influence of the Gulf Stream.
- Winter is just as dark as everywhere in these latitudes, and temperatures can (very rarely) reach -30°C in the south and even dip down to −50°C (−60°F) in the north, with 0 to −25°C (+35 to −15°F) being normal in the south.
- The brief Finnish summer is pleasant, with day temperatures around +15 to +25°C (on occasion up to +35°C), and is generally the best time of year to visit.
- Due to the extreme latitude, Finland experiences the famous Midnight sun near the summer solstice, when (if above the Arctic Circle) the sun never sets during the night and even in southern Finland it never really gets dark.
