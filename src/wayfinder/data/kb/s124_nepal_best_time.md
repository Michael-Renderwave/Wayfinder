## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** December, February, November — best overall: **December**.
- **Shoulder:** January, April, March.
- **Off-season:** October, May, June, July, August, September.
- Climate reference point: **Kathmandu** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Nepal experiences four seasons a year, summer, rainy season (or monsoon), autumn and winter.
- Monsoon rains in Nepal occur between June and September.
- In the Kathmandu Valley and Pokhara, monsoon rains typically consist of an hour or two of rain every two or three days.
- There is little dust in the air during autumn (October–December), so this is the best season to visit the hills and mountains.
