## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** August, May, September — best overall: **August**.
- **Shoulder:** July, October, June.
- **Off-season:** March, April, November, January, February, December.
- Climate reference point: **Maseru** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- The rainy season extends from October to April in which Lesotho gets 70 mm of rainfall, mostly during severe thunderstorms.
- Extensive snow falls are possible in winter but may occur in any month on the high mountains.
- Night time temperatures go below freezing in winter (May — September), and houses do not feature central heating, so bring a jacket.
- Because of the high altitude, the air is cold and dry, so bring moisturiser and a chap-stick.
