## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** July, August, June — best overall: **July**.
- **Shoulder:** September, October, May.
- **Off-season:** April, November, March, December, January, February.
- Climate reference point: **Jerusalem** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Summer in Israel (mid-June to mid-October) is characterized by high temperatures throughout the country, which will make staying outside unbearable, and you will only want to find yourself in the water, or in an air-conditioned place.
- Winter (December, January, February), can be characterized by beautiful days with 20 degrees Celsius, or days of heavy rain.
- Mountainous areas, especially Jerusalem, are common with temperatures of less than 10 degrees Celsius.
- For these reasons, the months with the most travelers (excluding periods with security problems) are April, May, October and November.
