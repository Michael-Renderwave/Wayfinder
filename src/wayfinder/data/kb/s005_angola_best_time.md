## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** July, June, August — best overall: **July**.
- **Shoulder:** September, October, May.
- **Off-season:** February, January, March, November, December, April.

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Like the rest of tropical Africa, Angola experiences distinct, alternating rainy and dry seasons.
- There is a short rainy season lasting from February to April.
- The northern part has a cool, dry season (May to October) and a hot, rainy season (November to April).
- The interior highlands have a mild climate with a rainy season from November through April followed by a cool dry season from May to October.
