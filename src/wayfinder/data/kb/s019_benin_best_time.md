## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** January, December, February — best overall: **January**.
- **Shoulder:** November, August, March.
- **Off-season:** April, July, June, September, October, May.
- Climate reference point: **Porto-Novo** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- The rainy period in the subequatorial north runs from March until October.
- The best time of the year to visit the country is from November to February, when the temperature moderates, and the weather is dry with low humidity.
