## Overview
Time-warped Cuba: Havana's pastel colonial core and classic cars, Trinidad's cobblestones, Viñales tobacco hills, and Caribbean beaches — with a strong, self-contained culture.

## Where to go
Top places: Havana, Varadero, Trinidad, Santiago de Cuba, Viñales.

## Landmarks & sights
- Trinidad Old Town (UNESCO)
- Viñales Valley tobacco hills

## Food & drink
- ropa vieja
- moros y cristianos
- fresh fish & shellfish
- rum, cigars & strong coffee

## Things to do
- classic-car tours of Havana
- salsa dancing
- Viñales tobacco farm visits
- Trinidad's cobblestones

## Where to stay
- Varadero (resorts)
Stay options: Havana Centro Hotel, Varadero Beach Resort.

## Good to know
- Best: November–April (dry)
- US travelers: check current travel rules; carry cash (EUR/GBP work best)
