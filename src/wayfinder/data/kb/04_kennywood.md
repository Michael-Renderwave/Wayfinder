Kennywood is a 122-acre, family-owned amusement park on the banks of the Allegheny River in West Mifflin, Pennsylvania, just north of Pittsburgh. The 2026 season runs from early May through early September, with extended hours on Fridays and Saturdays.

Signature rides include Racer (one of the world's fastest side-by-side wooden coasters), The Beast (steel coaster), Phoenix, Sky Rocket, Woodstock Express, Vortex, The Fury 3, and a full family area with kiddie coasters. Halloween Haunt runs select weekends in October with live haunted mazes.

Discounts and deals for 2026: Pittsburgh CityPASS includes Kennywood admission plus three other attractions for about $99 (saves ~40%); students with valid ID get roughly $10 off at the gate; seniors 62+ are discounted; and off-peak weekday pricing (Mon–Thu, June–August) is about $55–$65 for adults versus $75–$85 on weekends. Kids under 3 are free.

Plan on a full day (8+ hours). Parking is free for the first 30 minutes, then about $10–$15 all day.
