## Overview
A quiet, wine-rich country in the heart of Eastern Europe: Chisinau's central plaza, the ancient city of Orheiul Vechi, and some of the world's largest wine caves (Cricova, Milestii Mici).

## Where to go
Top places: Chisinau, Orheiul Vechi, Soroca, Cricova wine caves.

## Landmarks & sights
- Orheiul Vechi (ancient city)
- the Cricova wine caves
- the Soroca fortress

## Food & drink
- mamaliga (corn porridge)
- sarmale (cabbage rolls)
- local wine (Fetesti)
- placinte (pastry)

## Things to do
- wine tours (Cricova, Milestii Mici)
- the villages
- hiking (Orhei)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Chisinau City Center Hotel, Orhei Old Town Hotel.

## Good to know
- The wine is cheap and good
- Summer: the villages and the wine region
