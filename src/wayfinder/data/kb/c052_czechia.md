## Overview
Prague's fairy-tale center, Český Krumlov's castle over the Vltava, a world of castles and beer halls — and the great Central European spa towns, still working.

## Where to go
Top places: Prague, Český Krumlov, Karlštejn, Brno, spa towns.

## Landmarks & sights
- Prague Castle & Charles Bridge
- Český Krumlov old town (UNESCO)
- Karlštejn Castle
- Astronomical Clock (Prague)

## Food & drink
- goulash
- trdelník
- Pilsner & craft beer

## Things to do
- beer halls (Plzeň)
- Vltava cycling
- castle hopping (Karlštejn, Hrádek)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Prague Old Town Hotel, Prague Castle View Hotel, Český Krumlov Riverside Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer is peak in Prague's Old Town — the spa towns are quieter and cheaper
- The Czech koruna is not the euro
