## Overview
A small, scenic, Alpine country: Ljubljana's compact center, the island and castle of Lake Bled, the turquoise Soča valley, and the old town of the Piran on the coast.

## Where to go
Top places: Ljubljana, Lake Bled, Soča valley, Piran, castles.

## Landmarks & sights
- Lake Bled (island & castle)
- the Soča valley (turquoise river)
- the Piran (old town)
- the Ljubljana Castle

## Food & drink
- potica (honey cake)
- the local cheese
- fresh seafood (the Piran)
- the local wine (the hills)

## Things to do
- Lake Bled (the island)
- the Soča valley (kayaking, hiking)
- the Piran (the old town)
- the Ljubljana (the center)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Ljubljana City Center Hotel, Bled Lake Hotel, Piran Old Town Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer: the Soča valley; the high season is June–August
- Lake Bled is the highlight — book early
