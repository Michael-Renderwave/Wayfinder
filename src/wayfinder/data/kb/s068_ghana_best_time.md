## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** January, December, February — best overall: **January**.
- **Shoulder:** August, March, July.
- **Off-season:** November, April, September, May, June, October.
- Climate reference point: **Accra** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Average daily temperatures range from 30°C (86°F) during the day to 24°C (75°F) at night.
- In northern Ghana, rainy season occurs between April and October, while in the south of the country, rainfall is experienced between April and June and the months of September and October.
- Southwest Ghana tends to get wetter than the rest of the country.
- Between December and March, Ghana is affected by the Harmattan, a dry wind coming from the Sahara, causing the humidity to be very low and the air very dry and with dusty fog.
