## Overview
A country of three coasts (Caribbean, Pacific, plus the Amazon): Cartagena's walls, Medellín's reinvention, coffee hills, and world-class diving.

## Where to go
Top places: Bogotá, Cartagena, Medellín, San Andrés, Salento, Tayrona.

## Landmarks & sights
- Cartagena walled Old Town (UNESCO)
- Cerro La Popa
- Coffee Region landscapes

## Food & drink
- arepas
- bandeja paisa
- ajiaco (Bogotá chicken soup)
- sancocho
- fresh coconut & chicha

## Things to do
- Cartagena's colonial streets & islands
- Medellín street art & metro
- diving San Andrés

## Where to stay

Stay options: Cartagena Old Town Hotel, Medellín El Poblado Hotel.

## Good to know
- Medellín is spring-like year-round (elevation)
- Best beach season: December–April
