## Best time to visit

- **Off-season:** January, July, November, December, June, August.

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Nauru
 

A small, flat island almost on the Equator, Nauru is a textbook example of tropical climate.
- The temperature is constant around the year, with even the record lows and highs per month staying within a couple of degrees.
- Nauru is best avoided during the rainy season, which is from November to February.
- Even though full-fledged cyclones are rare at Naurus latitude, the sky is constantly cloudy and torrential rains and thunderstorms are frequent during this time of the year.
