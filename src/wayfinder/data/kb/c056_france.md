## Overview
Europe's classic: Paris' museums and cafés, the lavender markets of Provence, Mont Saint-Michel, the Loire châteaux, and the sun-and-cliff drama of the Côte d'Azur.

## Where to go
Top places: Paris, Côte d'Azur, Marseille & Provence, Dordogne, Normandy, Alps.

## Landmarks & sights
- Eiffel Tower & the Louvre
- Mont Saint-Michel
- the canals of Annecy

## Food & drink
- croissants & baguettes
- bouillabaisse (Marseille)
- cassoulet

## Things to do
- Provence markets
- Loire Valley châteaux
- Alsace cycling

## Where to stay

Stay options: Nice Old Town Hotel, Tours Loire Valley Hotel.

## Good to know
- Many towns empty in August — the south is quieter
- The TGV train makes day trips easy
