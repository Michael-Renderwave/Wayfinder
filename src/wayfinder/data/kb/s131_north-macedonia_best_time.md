## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** July, August, June — best overall: **July**.
- **Shoulder:** September, October, April.
- **Off-season:** May, March, February, December, January, November.
- Climate reference point: **Skopje** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- North Macedonia has warm, dry summers and autumns, and relatively cold winters with heavy snowfall.
