## Overview
Central America's smallest country: Pacific surf, smokestack volcanoes, pupusas, and colonial towns — a compact, affordable trip.

## Where to go
Top places: San Salvador, Surf City, Santa Ana, Lake Ilopango, San Miguel.

## Landmarks & sights
- Santa Ana Volcano (smokestack)
- Bejuco Beach
- El Boquerón market (San Miguel)

## Food & drink
- pupusas (stuffed corn cakes)
- yuca frita
- pastelitos
- Salvadoran chocolate & vanilla

## Things to do
- surfing (Beaches, El Zonte)
- volcano hikes (Santa Ana)
- coffee farm tours
- colonial San Miguel

## Where to stay

Stay options: San Salvador City Hotel, Surf City Beach Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best: December–April (dry)
- Small country — most of it fits in 3–5 days
