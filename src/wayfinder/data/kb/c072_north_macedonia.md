## Overview
A small Balkan country of the deep, clear Lake Ohrid, the Matka canyon, and the Galicica mountains — a quiet, affordable, lake-and-mountain base.

## Where to go
Top places: Skopje, Lake Ohrid, Matka canyon, Galicica.

## Landmarks & sights
- Lake Ohrid (endemic fish)
- the Matka canyon
- the Galicica National Park

## Food & drink
- tavche gravche (beans)
- fresh trout (Ohrid)
- local cheese (kajmak)
- rakija

## Things to do
- the lake (Ohrid)
- kayaking (the Matka)
- hiking (the Galicica)
- beaches (Ohrid)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Ohrid Lakeside Hotel, Skopje City Center Hotel.

## Good to know
- Summer: Lake Ohrid is the highlight
- The lake is the jewel — the beaches are quiet
