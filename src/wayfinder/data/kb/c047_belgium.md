## Overview
A dense, easy weekend country: Brussels' Grand-Place and waffles, Bruges' canals, Ghent's student charm, and the world's best beer and chocolate — all within a few hours by train.

## Where to go
Top places: Brussels, Bruges, Ghent, Antwerp, Ardennes.

## Landmarks & sights
- Bruges belfry & canals (UNESCO)
- Grand-Place (Brussels, UNESCO)
- Atomium
- Ghent's Graslei waterfront

## Food & drink
- mussels & fries
- waterzooi (chicken/vegetable stew)
- Belgian chocolate & waffles
- lamb with mash (waterzooi style)

## Things to do
- canal cycling (Bruges–Ghent)
- beer tastings & breweries
- chocolate tours
- beaches at Ostend

## Where to stay

Stay options: Brussels Grand Place Hotel, Bruges Old Town Hotel, Ghent Riverside Hotel.

## Good to know
- Brussels is best by tram — traffic is heavy
- Many shops close Sundays
