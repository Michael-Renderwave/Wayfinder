## Overview
A compact Andean country with one of the world's greatest wildlife destinations — the Galápagos — plus volcanoes, equator markers, and Amazonian rainforest.

## Where to go
Top places: Quito, Guayaquil, Galápagos, Baños, Cotopaxi.

## Landmarks & sights
- Galápagos wildlife (UNESCO)
- Mitad del Mundo (equator)
- Cotopaxi volcano
- Quito Old Town (UNESCO)

## Food & drink
- encebollado (shrimp soup)
- ceviche
- locro (corn & potato)
- canelazo (cinnamon-cane drink)

## Things to do
- Galápagos wildlife (penguins, tortoises)
- volcano hikes (Cotopaxi)
- hot springs & rafting (Baños)
- Amazon day trips (Tayta)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Quito City Center Hotel, Galápagos Base Hotel, Baños Hot Spring Hotel.

## Good to know
- Galápagos: any month works — book the inter-island flights early
- Mainland dry season: May–September
