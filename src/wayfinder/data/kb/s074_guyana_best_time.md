## Best time to visit
- **Ideal months:** March, September, October — best overall: **March**.
- **Shoulder:** January, February, April.
- **Off-season:** August, November, May, June, July, December.
- Climate reference point: **Georgetown** (2023-2025 normals).

## Why — what travelers say (Wikivoyage, summarized)
- Guyanas climate is tropical with the hot, humid conditions moderated by northeast trade winds.
- There are two distinct rainy seasons: May to mid-August and mid-November to mid-January.
- Flash floods are a constant threat during these rainy seasons.
