## Overview
A nature giant: Rocky-Mountain parks, Pacific coasts, five Great Lakes, and cities like Vancouver, Toronto, and Montreal — wide open space with big-city energy.

## Where to go
Top places: Ottawa, Vancouver, Toronto, Banff & Jasper, Montreal, Quebec City, Niagara Falls, Halifax.

## Landmarks & sights
- CN Tower (Toronto)
- Lake Louise (Banff)
- Niagara Falls
- Saguenay Fjord
- Gros Morne (Newfoundland)

## Food & drink
- poutine
- fresh Pacific salmon
- tourtière (meat pie)
- Nanaimo bars

## Things to do
- whale watching (Vancouver)
- Northern Lights (Yellowknife, Jasper)
- maple sugaring season
- cross-country skiing

## Where to stay

Stay options: Vancouver City Center Hotel, Banff Lakeside Hotel, Toronto Airport Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best outdoors: June–September
- Fall colors peak mid-October in the Rockies
