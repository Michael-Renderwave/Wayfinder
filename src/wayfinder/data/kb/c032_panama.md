## Overview
A bridge continent: the Canal, Bocas del Toro islands, San Blas' Guna villages, and Boquete's cloud forest — tropical variety in one country.

## Where to go
Top places: Panama City, Bocas del Toro, San Blas, Boquete, Santa Catalina.

## Landmarks & sights
- Panama Canal (Miraflores locks)
- Casco Viejo (UNESCO)
- Bocas del Toro archipelago

## Food & drink
- ceviche
- sancocho
- yuca with pork
- fresh seafood & cafecito

## Things to do
- Canal transit at Miraflores
- island hopping (Bocas)
- cloud-forest hikes (Boquete)
- surfing (Santa Catalina)
- wildlife (sloths, monkeys)

## Where to stay

Stay options: Panama City Casco Viejo Hotel, Bocas del Toro Island Hotel, Boquete Cloud Forest Hotel.

## Good to know
- Best: December–April (dry)
- Cheap domestic flights connect the islands
